### User-directed structural recomposition — 2026-09-05

The previous five-section `primitive`-led order has been superseded by an explicit user-directed four-part architecture. The current canonical section headings are:

1. `Exchange, Kula, and Mutual Calibration, 1926–1929`
2. `Travel and Comparative Method, 1929–1931`
3. `Institutions: Leiden, Anthropology, and the Human Sciences, 1931`
4. `1933 as a Hinge: Solutions, Limits, and Divergence`

Structural logic:

- **exchange first:** America is now the encounter scene rather than a standalone `primitive` module; the Trekvaart conversation, three-book dispatch including *Argonauts*, reciprocal reading, Trobriands, and Kula carry the direct pair argument;
- **travel second:** compare knowledge practices rather than itineraries. Current controlled evidence supports Malinowski's Trobriand fieldwork versus Huizinga's Java–Bali–Hong Kong travel / memory / card / source-verification regime. No controlled evidence presently supports a shared Bali destination, so do not force geographic symmetry;
- **institutions third:** Leiden now carries the wider institutional history of anthropology / human sciences, including museums, philanthropy, disciplinary organization, `primitive or semi-cultured races`, Malinowski's audit and Huizinga's outsiderhood;
- **1933 as hinge, not coda or completion:** compare their incomplete responses. Kula → potlatch → Burgundy demonstrates comparison through specified relations; Academy `refrained`, Hofstra, and the persistence of developmental language prevent a conversion or disappearance story. A symmetrical post-1933 account is not yet source-controlled and should not be invented.

The 2026-09-05 Draft 04 recomposition also removed the body paragraph whose main job was lexical polysemy (`Trois esprits prégothiques` + the Renaissance `primitief` packet) and compressed the Philip-the-Good portrait dossier into a contrastive source-practice example.
