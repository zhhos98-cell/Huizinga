# Only a Benevolent Outsider / 只是一个善意的局外人
## Johan Huizinga, Bronisław Malinowski, and the Waning of the Primitive, 1926–1933 / 约翰·赫伊津哈、布罗尼斯拉夫·马林诺夫斯基与“原始”的消退，1926–1933

By August 1931, five years after their American encounter, Johan Huizinga had read Bronisław Malinowski for years, exchanged work with him, and helped frame a proposal for a Centre of Anthropological Research at Leiden. Asked who could give the centre a ‘central aim or principle’, he answered: ‘I myself am only a benevolent outsider.’[^1]

到1931年8月，在两人于美国相遇五年之后，约翰·赫伊津哈已经阅读布罗尼斯拉夫·马林诺夫斯基多年，与他交换著作，并参与拟定莱顿“人类学研究中心”的方案。当被问及谁能够赋予这个中心一个“核心目标或原则”时，他回答道：“我自己不过是一个善意的局外人。”[^1]

The phrase is harder to place than it looks. Huizinga had used Malinowski in teaching and medieval history; Malinowski had read Huizinga and praised his historical approach; Huizinga was now assembling an anthropological project. Why did proximity produce an outsider?

这句话比看上去更难安放。赫伊津哈已经在教学和中世纪史研究中使用马林诺夫斯基；马林诺夫斯基也读过赫伊津哈，并赞赏他的历史研究方法；而此刻赫伊津哈又正在组织一个人类学项目。为什么如此接近，反而产生了一个“局外人”？

*Primitive* is part of the problem, though not as one stable concept shared by both men. Malinowski wrote of savage and primitive societies; Huizinga of ‘so-called primitive culture’, a ‘stage of savage life’, and *primitief* in quite different historical and philological settings. What joined these uses was not a common definition but a recurring problem of historical inference: what could relations observed among peoples described as primitive disclose about other times and forms of life? Malinowski increasingly answered by unpacking such categories into reciprocity, obligation, knowledge and practice; Huizinga asked what those relations could bear when carried into historical comparison. The word travelled easily. What it could authorize became harder to assume.

*Primitive* 构成了这个问题的一部分，但它并不是两人共同拥有的一个稳定概念。马林诺夫斯基谈论 savage 与 primitive societies；赫伊津哈则在完全不同的历史学和语文学语境中谈到“所谓的原始文化”、“野蛮生活的阶段”，以及荷兰语 *primitief*。把这些用法连接起来的并不是一个共同定义，而是一个反复出现的历史推论问题：从那些被称为 primitive 的人群中观察到的关系，究竟能够揭示其他时代和生活形式的什么？马林诺夫斯基越来越倾向于把这类范畴拆解为互惠、义务、知识和实践；赫伊津哈则追问，当这些关系被带入历史比较时，它们究竟能够承担多少解释工作。这个词很容易流动；它究竟能够授权什么判断，却越来越难以被理所当然地预设。

This article deliberately borrows Huizinga’s own language of waning as a second-order historical term. The borrowing comes from the self-reflexive cautions in the 1919 preface to *Herfsttij*. Huizinga warned that attention fixed on decline could cast ‘te veel van de schaduw des doods’ over the work, and ended by asking whether historical inquiry could approach ‘den wezenlijken inhoud ... die in die vormen heeft gerust’ (Huizinga, 1919). He had opened the same preface by criticizing the habit of viewing each period above all for the promises it held for the next. *Waning* here names a problem of precursor history, death-shadow, and the relation between forms and what they contain.

本文有意借用赫伊津哈自己的“waning（消退／衰退）”语言，把它作为一个二阶的历史术语。这个借用来自1919年 *Herfsttij* 序言中赫伊津哈对历史书写本身的反思性警告。他提醒说，若把注意力固定在衰落之上，就可能把“过多死亡的阴影”（*te veel van de schaduw des doods*）投射到研究对象上；在序言末尾，他又追问历史研究是否能够接近“曾经安置于这些形式之中的真正内容”（*den wezenlijken inhoud ... die in die vormen heeft gerust*）（Huizinga, 1919）。而在同一序言开头，他已经批评了一种习惯：主要根据一个时代为下一个时代预示了什么来理解它。这里的 *waning* 指向的，是前驱史、死亡阴影，以及形式与其所承载内容之间关系的问题。

Writing *Mijn weg tot de historie* in 1943, Huizinga offered a second retrospective perspective. He remembered reading and excerpting Max Müller as unquestionable wisdom before Tylor’s *Primitive Culture* opened vistas that remained ‘kiemen van mijn wetenschappelijk denken’, even though its animist thesis had long become obsolete and been abandoned (Huizinga, 1947). An intellectual form could lose doctrinal authority without disappearing from a later repertoire.

1943年撰写 *Mijn weg tot de historie* 时，赫伊津哈提供了第二个回顾性的视角。他记得自己曾把马克斯·缪勒当作毋庸置疑的智慧来阅读和摘录，随后泰勒的 *Primitive Culture* 又打开了新的视野；即使其中的万物有灵论命题早已过时并被放弃，其中某些东西仍然是他“科学思想的种子”（*kiemen van mijn wetenschappelijk denken*）（Huizinga, 1947）。一种思想形式可以失去教义上的权威，却仍然存在于后来的知识储备之中。

Recent scholarship shows that the interwar *primitive* was not fading into obscurity. The emerging human sciences made it increasingly visible and operational: in books and courses, disputes over law and mentality, philanthropic programmes, museum classification and institutional design (Biehn, 2009; Chimisso, 2000; Pels, 2022). Recent work on Malinowski has similarly weakened the familiar story of a sudden passage from evolutionism to functionalism, describing a refocusing towards intensive local study rather than a disciplinary reset (Lagerspetz and Suolinna, 2025). The question here is what the surviving category could still settle without further relations, evidence and disciplinary authority. The Huizinga–Malinowski exchange shifts that problem sideways, from succession within anthropology to movement between anthropology and history. The question could travel more easily than the support for an answer.

近年的研究表明，两次世界大战之间的 *primitive* 并没有淡出视野。相反，新兴的人文科学使它越来越可见、越来越具有操作性：它出现在著作和课程中，出现在关于法律与心智的争论中，出现在慈善基金项目、博物馆分类和制度设计中（Biehn, 2009; Chimisso, 2000; Pels, 2022）。近期关于马林诺夫斯基的研究同样削弱了那种从进化论突然转向功能主义的熟悉叙事，更倾向于把这一变化描述为对密集地方研究的重新聚焦，而不是一次学科重置（Lagerspetz and Suolinna, 2025）。这里的问题是：在这个范畴继续存活的情况下，如果没有进一步的关系、证据与学科权威，它还能够独自裁定什么？赫伊津哈与马林诺夫斯基的往来把这个问题横向移动了：它不再只是人类学内部一种理论接替另一种理论的问题，而成为知识在人类学与历史学之间移动的问题。问题本身比支持某个答案的依据更容易流动。

That shift exposes a gap in the literature. Most accounts stop at the disciplinary edge. They explain how anthropology reworked its own *primitive*; they say less about what happened when its questions entered another historical craft whose sources, comparisons and claims to authority were organized differently. The encounter matters precisely because neither man entered it as a stranger to *primitive*. The term belonged to a wider human-scientific atmosphere, but a shared vocabulary did not make its uses identical. This article follows the category at the scale of situations—book, course, letter, museum memorandum, historical comparison—and asks how Huizinga and Malinowski themselves qualified, redirected or withheld the claims that *primitive* invited.

这种横向移动暴露出文献中的一个缺口。多数研究在学科边缘处止步：它们解释人类学如何改造自身的 *primitive*，却较少讨论，当这些问题进入另一种历史学技艺——一种对史料、比较和权威主张有着不同组织方式的技艺——时，会发生什么。两人的相遇之所以重要，正是因为他们都不是作为 *primitive* 的陌生人进入这段关系的。这个词属于更广泛的人文科学氛围，但共享词汇并不意味着共享用法。本文把这一范畴放在具体情境的尺度上追踪——书、课程、信件、博物馆备忘录、历史比较——并考察赫伊津哈与马林诺夫斯基如何各自限定、重定向，或拒绝 *primitive* 所诱发的判断。

Recent Huizinga scholarship has also insisted on putting his historical work back inside the pressures of his own present. Rydin (2024) reads that work through interwar rupture and loss; the pair archive extends the historicization into the reorganizing human sciences, where books, foundations, museums and disciplinary projects became sites of choice. Recent intellectual history has already moved from older models of origins and influence toward reception, circulation and active reuse (Wickberg, 2023); work on the ‘(non-)globalisation’ of ideas has gone further by treating actors’ decisions to appropriate or not appropriate an idea as causal historical action (Morgan, 2025). The present case applies that sensitivity to a disciplinary boundary: a transfer deliberately not completed is part of the exchange, not an evidentiary absence.

近期赫伊津哈研究也坚持把他的历史著作重新放回其自身时代的压力之中。Rydin（2024）从战间期的断裂与失落来理解这些作品；两人的往来档案则把这种历史化推进到正在重组的人文科学之中，在那里，书籍、基金会、博物馆与学科项目本身都成为选择发生的场所。近期思想史已经从较旧的“起源—影响”模型转向接受、流通与主动再使用（Wickberg, 2023）；关于观念“（非）全球化”的研究更进一步，把行动者选择采纳或不采纳某种观念本身视为具有因果意义的历史行动（Morgan, 2025）。本文把这种敏感性用于一条学科边界：一项被有意保留为未完成状态的转移，本身就是交换的一部分，而不是证据的空缺。

## America, 1926 / 美国，1926

During Huizinga’s spring 1926 American tour, his itinerary carried him through Philadelphia, Baltimore, Washington and Chapel Hill. At Harvard his route crossed Malinowski’s: Malinowski was himself in the United States on a Rockefeller-supported visit, and his report places Huizinga in a seminar on ‘the functional view of Anthropology’.[^2]

1926年春赫伊津哈赴美旅行期间，他先后经过费城、巴尔的摩、华盛顿和教堂山。在哈佛，他的行程与马林诺夫斯基交汇：马林诺夫斯基本人也正在美国进行一次由洛克菲勒资助的访问，而他的报告明确把赫伊津哈列为一场讨论“人类学的功能观点”的研讨会参与者。[^2]

The encounter met an existing repertoire. Huizinga later remembered reaching history from Sanskrit and comparative linguistics by a ‘salto mortale’; he also admitted that his early teaching preferred a vivid story to critical groundedness (Huizinga, 1947). In contemporary texts, he had already distinguished geological or biological *ontwikkeling* from ‘de functie van het historisch begrijpen’ in 1921, testing historical schemes by what they allowed one to understand rather than by chronology alone (Huizinga, 1921). On 7 April 1926, roughly a week before his 13/14 April departure for America, he attacked *evolutie* as a general historical principle, defined cultural history as social morphology concerned with what connects people to one another rather than with what holds them together internally, and placed ethnology among the special cultural sciences already practising such morphology (Huizinga, 1927: 5–7). His Indian papers also used *primitief* for earlier intellectual or medical layers.[^3]

这次相遇面对的并不是一片空白，而是一套已经存在的知识储备。赫伊津哈后来回忆说，自己从梵文和比较语言学进入历史学，几乎是一次“致命跳跃”（*salto mortale*）；他也承认，早期教学时自己更偏爱鲜活的叙述，而不太重视批判性的根据（Huizinga, 1947）。在同时代文本中，他早在1921年就已经把地质学或生物学意义上的 *ontwikkeling* 与“历史理解的功能”（*de functie van het historisch begrijpen*）区分开来，检验历史图式的标准不是年代次序本身，而是它们究竟使人能够理解什么（Huizinga, 1921）。1926年4月7日，大约在4月13/14日启程赴美前一周，他又批评把 *evolutie* 当作一种普遍历史原则，把文化史定义为一种社会形态学，关注的是人与人之间相互联结的东西，而不是单纯从内部维系一个群体的东西，并把民族学列为已经在实践这种形态学的特殊文化科学之一（Huizinga, 1927: 5–7）。他的印度研究材料中也已用 *primitief* 指称较早的思想或医学层次。[^3]

On Malinowski’s side, the Rockefeller report gives the seminar a clearer place in his own programme. It linked anthropology to the practical influence of social science on policy and to the place of science in American life, and under ‘The functional view of Anthropology’ described ideas, customs and institutions through the work they performed within an integral cultural scheme. The report says that similar theoretical discussions were repeated at Harvard. By 19 May, writing home from Boston, Huizinga was already calling him ‘mijn nieuwen vriend Malinowski’ and promising to tell his son more about him after returning.[^2]

从马林诺夫斯基这一边看，洛克菲勒报告使这场研讨会在他自己的计划中位置更加清楚。报告把人类学与社会科学对政策的实际影响、以及科学在美国生活中的位置联系起来；在“人类学的功能观点”一节中，它通过观念、习俗和制度在一个整体文化结构中实际发挥的作用来描述它们。报告说，类似的理论讨论后来又在哈佛举行。到5月19日，赫伊津哈从波士顿写信回家时，已经把他称作“我的新朋友马林诺夫斯基”（*mijnen nieuwen vriend Malinowski*），并答应回家后再向儿子详细讲述这个人。[^2]

Histories of interwar anthropology place such encounters inside foundation patronage, where anthropologists had to articulate the distinctiveness of their objects and methods (Biehn, 2009). Five years later Malinowski would ask what could make Leiden’s resources anthropology.

关于战间期人类学的历史研究把这类相遇置于基金会资助的环境中；在这种环境里，人类学家必须明确说明自己的研究对象与方法究竟有何独特之处（Biehn, 2009）。五年之后，马林诺夫斯基会反过来追问：莱顿现有的那些资源，究竟凭什么能够构成人类学？

## Books and Letters, 1926–1929 / 书籍与通信，1926–1929

By December, nearly seven months after the Boston letter, Huizinga was recalling another conversation about ‘ethnological problems’, this time along the Haarlemsche Trekvaart. That recollection appears in his letter of 12 December 1926. He thanked Malinowski for a letter followed by three books, ‘the small, the medium and the big one’, and recalled their earlier conversation while walking along the Haarlemsche Trekvaart. Huizinga began with *Crime and Custom in Savage Society*, made the calf joke, then turned serious: Malinowski’s ‘outlook on ethnological problems’ was ‘just the one I can profit by for my historical method’. Nineteen days later, in a second letter of 31 December, he made the affinity explicit: ‘We emphasize the functional side of cultural phenomena.’ Feudal law, troubadour culture and the efficacy of words followed immediately.[^4]

到12月，距离那封波士顿家书已经将近七个月，赫伊津哈又回忆起两人关于“民族学问题”的另一次谈话；这一次，地点是在 Haarlemsche Trekvaart 运河沿线。这一回忆出现在他1926年12月12日的信中。他感谢马林诺夫斯基先寄来一封信，随后又寄来三本书——“小的、中等的和大的那本”——并回想起两人此前沿 Haarlemsche Trekvaart 散步时的谈话。赫伊津哈先从 *Crime and Custom in Savage Society* 读起，开了那个“小牛”玩笑，随后转入严肃判断：马林诺夫斯基对“民族学问题的看法”，正是“我可以用来帮助自己历史方法的那一种”。十九天之后，在12月31日的第二封信中，他把这种亲近说得更明确：“我们强调文化现象的功能一面。”紧接着出现的，就是封建法律、行吟诗人文化以及语言的效力。[^4]

Malinowski’s published self-description adds another source. Earlier in 1926, in the dedication to *Myth in Primitive Psychology*, adapted from his 1925 Frazer Lecture, he recalled illness interrupting physical and chemical research, *The Golden Bough*, and becoming ‘bound to the service of Frazerian anthropology’ (Malinowski, 1926b: 5–6). His early writings place that conversion story inside a broader Central European formation (Thornton and Skalník, 1993). *Crime and Custom* still named ‘primitive law’, but its account of how rules bound moved through reciprocal services, kin relations, public knowledge and obligations of return (Malinowski, 1926a). His attack on ‘primitive mentality’ worked in a similar way: Trobriand agriculture, navigation and technical knowledge stood against allowing the label to determine the contents of mind in advance (Tilley, 2010). The interwar controversy over ‘primitive mentality’ was also a dispute over disciplinary competence: claims about the universality or transformation of mind were tied to claims about the scientific status of ethnology itself (Chimisso, 2000).

马林诺夫斯基公开发表的自我叙述提供了另一种材料。1926年稍早，在由1925年弗雷泽讲座改写而来的 *Myth in Primitive Psychology* 献辞中，他回忆自己如何因疾病中断物理与化学研究，随后读到 *The Golden Bough*，并从此“投身于弗雷泽式人类学的服务之中”（Malinowski, 1926b: 5–6）。他的早期著作把这个“皈依”故事放回一个更广泛的中欧思想形成之中（Thornton and Skalník, 1993）。*Crime and Custom* 仍然使用“primitive law”这个名称，但它真正说明规则如何具有约束力时，讨论的是互惠服务、亲属关系、公共知识，以及回报义务（Malinowski, 1926a）。他对“primitive mentality”的批评也以类似方式展开：特罗布里恩群岛的农业、航海与技术知识，构成了反对让一个总括标签预先决定心智内容的证据（Tilley, 2010）。战间期围绕“primitive mentality”的争论同时也是一场关于学科能力的争论：关于心智究竟普遍还是会发生转化的主张，与民族学能否成为一门科学的主张纠缠在一起（Chimisso, 2000）。

For their exchange, the important move was from what primitive or savage societies were said to be toward what relations observed there could do in another historical argument. Huizinga’s 31 December letter moved from ‘so-called primitive culture’ to vanity, generosity, friendship, words and feudal relations. Elsewhere a working slip called knightly games ‘beleving der Feodaltijd’.[^4] The slip shows the sort of historical form to which a question about social efficacy could attach. Malinowski’s ethnography supplied relations; Huizinga tested their historical reach.[^17]

对两人的往来而言，关键的移动，是从“primitive 或 savage societies 被说成是什么”，转向“在那里观察到的关系能够在另一种历史论证中做什么”。赫伊津哈12月31日的信从“所谓的原始文化”移向虚荣、慷慨、友谊、言语与封建关系。在其他工作笔记中，他又把骑士游戏称作“*beleving der Feodaltijd*”。这张纸条显示了一个关于社会效力的问题究竟可以依附于怎样的历史形式。马林诺夫斯基的民族志提供关系；赫伊津哈则检验这些关系在历史中的作用范围。[^17]

Fifteen months after the 31 December letter, on 29 March 1928, *Sex and Repression in Savage Society* gave Huizinga ‘new visions concerning the very origins of culture’, while *Crime and Custom* remained perhaps more useful ‘for historical use’.[^5] The distinction is revealing: one book promised an account of origins; the other offered relations that Huizinga thought he could carry into historical work. In the same letter he resisted one Freudian argument by invoking ‘the immense complexity of the smallest social phenomenon’. He then generalized about Freud and Marx through the antisemitic stereotype of a ‘Talmudic mind’. Complexity and flattening sat on the same page. The letter also sent Huizinga’s remarks on American culture to Malinowski for judgment.

在12月31日那封信十五个月之后，1928年3月29日，赫伊津哈说 *Sex and Repression in Savage Society* 使他对“文化最初的起源”产生了“新的视野”；而 *Crime and Custom* 对“历史上的使用”也许仍然更有帮助。这个区分很能说明问题：一本书承诺解释起源；另一本则提供了赫伊津哈认为可以带进历史研究中的关系。在同一封信中，他又用“最微小社会现象的巨大复杂性”来抵抗一种弗洛伊德式论证；随后却又通过反犹主义的“Talmudic mind”刻板印象，把弗洛伊德和马克思概括在一起。复杂化与扁平化出现在同一页上。这封信同时还把赫伊津哈关于美国文化的文字寄给马林诺夫斯基，请他判断。[^5]

Malinowski replied on 15 August, ‘We often speak of you’, and called the reading of Huizinga’s American notes ‘a most stimulating event’: ‘we read them aloud’.[^6] Nine months later, on 13 May 1929, after receiving *The Sexual Life of Savages in North-Western Melanesia*, Huizinga said he felt ‘quite at home in the Trobriands’. He then described the course he had taught the previous winter to students at Delft Technical University, organized around *homo faber*: it ran from ‘the stage of savage life’ through old Aryan culture to Rome and its decay.[^7] Thick familiarity and stadial order coexisted. The letters show no comparable attempt by Huizinga to reorganize Malinowski’s anthropology as a theory.

马林诺夫斯基8月15日回信说：“我们常常谈到你”，并把大家阅读赫伊津哈美国笔记称为“一件极其令人振奋的事”：“我们把它们读出了声。”九个月之后，1929年5月13日，收到 *The Sexual Life of Savages in North-Western Melanesia* 后，赫伊津哈说自己感觉“完全像在特罗布里恩群岛里一样自在”。随后他描述了前一个冬天给代尔夫特理工大学学生开的课程，这门课以 *homo faber* 为组织轴，从“野蛮生活的阶段”一路经过古老雅利安文化，讲到罗马及其衰败。高度熟悉与阶段秩序可以同时存在。现有信件中没有同等证据显示赫伊津哈曾试图反过来把马林诺夫斯基的人类学重组为一套理论。[^6][^7]

In Huizinga’s own methodological writing, 1929 adds a more general formulation. ‘The primitive cultures of the whole world’, he wrote, had become components of modern civilization; yet a history adequate to that civilization still had to answer to critically scientific verification (Huizinga, 1929: 156–168). In the same essay he divided the labour more exactly: ethnology and related sciences could define recurrent forms, while cultural history followed their changing operation in historical events; the boundary was crossed from both sides and retained its significance. Cultural history could learn from ‘de praktische sociologie der Amerikanen’ and from Mauss without becoming subordinate labour for sociology (Huizinga, 1929).

赫伊津哈1929年的方法论写作又给出了一个更一般的表达。他写道，“全世界的原始文化”已经成为现代文明的组成部分；然而，要书写与这种文明相称的历史，仍然必须接受批判性的科学验证（Huizinga, 1929: 156–168）。在同一篇文章中，他更精确地划分了工作：民族学以及相关科学可以界定反复出现的形式，而文化史则追踪这些形式在历史事件中如何以不同方式发生作用；这条边界可以从两边被跨越，但其意义仍然存在。文化史可以向“美国人的实践社会学”以及莫斯学习，却无需因此成为社会学的附庸劳动（Huizinga, 1929）。

## Travel and the Work of Comparison / 旅行与比较的工作

Between the May 1929 letter and the July 1931 Leiden proposal, Huizinga’s own comparative work left a different documentary trail. In November 1929, while working on Philip the Good, Huizinga asked the museum in Gotha for a good photograph of the duke’s portrait. The surrounding Burgundian dossier compares portrait copies and museum photographs; newly recovered autograph letters can correct an earlier historical image.[^10] Working notes from the same period distinguish collecting or establishing historical material from ordering it under a point of view.[^11]

在1929年5月的信与1931年7月的莱顿方案之间，赫伊津哈自己的比较研究留下了另一种文献轨迹。1929年11月，在研究“好人菲利普”时，他向哥达的博物馆索取一张公爵肖像的高质量照片。围绕勃艮第材料形成的档案中，可以看到他比较肖像摹本与博物馆照片；新近发现的亲笔信还可以纠正此前形成的历史形象。同一时期的工作笔记又把“收集或确立历史材料”与“按照某种观点对材料进行组织”区分开来。[^10][^11]

By 1930, in his French manuscript on twelfth-century intellectual life, *Trois esprits prégothiques*, *primitive* appears near *Cet élément ludique*; a few pages later the text refuses a simple Renaissance-precursor reading.[^9] A separate Renaissance packet begins ‘Met dien primitief begrip der Renaissance…’—*primitief* modifying a concept, not a people.[^3] The same lexical form could mark different relations without carrying one anthropology-derived meaning.

到1930年，在赫伊津哈讨论十二世纪知识生活的法语手稿 *Trois esprits prégothiques* 中，*primitive* 出现在 *Cet élément ludique* 附近；几页之后，文本又拒绝把相关人物简单理解为文艺复兴的前驱。另一份关于文艺复兴的材料则以“*Met dien primitief begrip der Renaissance…*”开头——这里 *primitief* 修饰的是一个“概念”，而不是某个民族。同一个词形可以标记不同关系，而不必携带一种由人类学统一派生出来的意义。[^9][^3]

The interval also included Huizinga’s 1930–31 journey to Java, Bali and Hong Kong. Writing retrospectively in *Mijn weg tot de historie*, he contrasted a journey remembered ‘entirely’ from memory after an injury to his right eye with the day-to-day notes of his 1926 American trip (Huizinga, 1947). The surviving American archive shows what those earlier ‘notes’ meant: cards on frontier history, Indian policy and the Ghost Dance lead into comparative prose on America and Europe.[^8] Malinowski could revisit living relations; Huizinga moved through notes, memory, cards, reproductions and surviving texts. Both made distant worlds portable, but not under the same checks.

这个时段也包括赫伊津哈1930–31年前往爪哇、巴厘与香港的旅行。在 *Mijn weg tot de historie* 中回顾这次旅程时，他把由于右眼受伤而“完全”依赖记忆保存下来的亚洲旅行，与1926年美国之行逐日留下的笔记进行对比（Huizinga, 1947）。现存的美国档案让我们看到那些早期“笔记”究竟是什么：关于边疆史、印第安政策和 Ghost Dance 的卡片，随后进入关于美国与欧洲的比较性文字。马林诺夫斯基可以重新回到仍在生活中的关系；赫伊津哈则通过笔记、记忆、卡片、复制品与存世文本来移动。两人都把遥远世界变成可以携带的知识对象，但他们所依靠的核验条件并不相同。[^8]

Two strands of Huizinga scholarship bear directly on this material. One concerns source practice: work on the making of *Herfsttij* has already made Huizinga’s selective source use and material organization objects of historiography (Small, 2019; van der Lem, 2019). The later archive extends that problem across cards, travel memory, photographs, autograph letters and surviving texts.

赫伊津哈研究中有两条路径与这些材料直接相关。第一条涉及史料实践：关于 *Herfsttij* 写作过程的研究已经把赫伊津哈选择性使用史料以及组织材料的方式本身变成了史学研究对象（Small, 2019; van der Lem, 2019）。较晚时期的档案把这个问题扩展到了卡片、旅行记忆、照片、亲笔信和存世文本之间。

A second concerns anthropology. Arnade (2019) emphasizes anthropological affinities in Huizinga’s treatment of ritual and cultural form; Vale (2021) warns that an *anthropologist avant la lettre* can obscure the philological and linguistic formation that continued to shape his historical work. The correspondence places direct anthropological exchange alongside the historian’s continuing philological checks. Taken together, the archive makes the intermediate work of comparison visible. *Primitive* remained portable; the basis for moving from one of its relations to another became less automatic.

第二条路径涉及人类学。Arnade（2019）强调赫伊津哈处理仪式与文化形式时所表现出的人类学亲近性；Vale（2021）则警告，把他称作一个“avant la lettre 的人类学家”，可能会遮蔽持续塑造其历史研究的语文学与语言学形成。两人的通信把直接的人类学交流，与这位历史学家持续进行的语文学核验并置在了一起。把这些材料放在一起，比较过程中间那些原本容易被隐藏的工作就变得可见。*Primitive* 仍然很容易被携带；但从这个词所包含的一种关系移动到另一种关系时，所需的根据却不再那么自动成立。

## Leiden, 1931 / 莱顿，1931

On 19 July 1931 Huizinga sent Malinowski ‘a paper which I made up’ for a Centre of Anthropological Research. The proposal had already circulated and been revised within Leiden, and followed a visit by Van Sickle and Tracy Kittredge, who had come ‘to look into the matter’.[^12] Leiden already possessed an ethnographical museum, Oriental and language institutes, legal and *adat* expertise tied to colonial administration, and other dispersed scholarly resources; Huizinga asked Malinowski for judgment and, if favourable, Rockefeller help. If Malinowski did not approve, Huizinga asked him to treat the request as though it had not been made. The supporting papers placed those resources on a civilizational map. Java could be ‘high culture’, Bali an unusually interesting Hindu remnant, other populations a ‘backward stage’. One annex went further: ‘Ethnographical objects as data for psychological research about primitive or semi-cultured races’.[^13] Wicker-work, textiles, metalwork and carving could become evidence of mental capacities; those capacities could bear on development, administration and indigenous industry. Pels’s work on classification helps situate this: categories could describe objects, organize research and structure intervention at the same time (Pels, 2022).

1931年7月19日，赫伊津哈把一份为“人类学研究中心”写成的“我拼出来的文件”寄给马林诺夫斯基。这份方案此前已经在莱顿内部流转并被修改过，而且是在 Van Sickle 与 Tracy Kittredge 来访、专门“了解此事”之后寄出的。莱顿已经拥有民族学博物馆、东方学与语言研究机构、与殖民行政相联系的法律和 *adat* 专门知识，以及其他分散的学术资源；赫伊津哈请马林诺夫斯基判断，并在认可的情况下争取洛克菲勒支持。如果马林诺夫斯基不赞成，赫伊津哈请他把这次请求当作从未发生过。配套文件把这些资源放在一张文明地图上：爪哇可以被称为“high culture”，巴厘是一个异常有趣的印度教遗存，其他人口则处于“backward stage”。其中一份附件更进一步，题为：“民族志物件作为研究 primitive 或 semi-cultured races 心理的数据。”藤编、纺织品、金属制品与雕刻都可以成为心智能力的证据；而这些能力又可以与发展、行政和本土工业相联系。Pels 关于分类的研究有助于定位这一点：范畴可以同时描述对象、组织研究，并构造干预的方式（Pels, 2022）。[^12][^13]

Huizinga’s own 1930 lectures set a limit from another direction. In a lecture sequence on seventeenth-century Dutch civilization he admitted social, economic, political and, ‘if one wishes’, ethnographic conditions, then denied that their enumeration completely explained a civilization.[^11]

赫伊津哈自己的1930年讲座从另一个方向给出限制。在一组关于十七世纪荷兰文明的讲座中，他承认社会、经济、政治以及——“如果你愿意这么说”——民族志条件都可以成立；但他随后否认，单纯把这些条件逐项列出，就能完整解释一种文明。[^11]

Eleven days after Huizinga sent the memorandum, Malinowski replied on 30 July. Van Sickle had already spoken to him about Leiden during a recent weekend stay, and before seeing the memorandum Malinowski had expressed unreserved interest and enthusiasm. After reading Huizinga’s paper he still said he would gladly do what he could and intended to write to the Rockefeller side, but postponed that intervention until Huizinga had answered ‘a few comments on your Memorandum’. Who would ‘carry on the work and inspire the institution with their spirit’? What ‘central aim or principle’ would hold it together? He worried about museum buildings and equipment dominating the scheme, rejected anthropology as a *mixtum compositum* of archaeology, collectioneering and other activities, asked for ‘a definite social science’, urged Leiden to ‘sociologise’, and joined living language to living culture. His judgment of Huizinga himself was equally explicit: ‘Your method in history strikes me as being the right sort of really anthropological approach, so that if you could play even a teaching part it would be invaluable.’[^14]

赫伊津哈寄出备忘录十一天之后，马林诺夫斯基于7月30日回信。Van Sickle 最近一次周末停留时已经向他谈过莱顿；在真正看到备忘录之前，马林诺夫斯基曾毫无保留地表示兴趣和热情。读完赫伊津哈的文件后，他仍说自己乐意尽力，并计划给洛克菲勒方面写信，但他把这一步暂时推迟到赫伊津哈回应他“关于你们备忘录的几点意见”之后。谁将“实际承担工作，并用他们的精神给这个机构以生命”？什么“核心目标或原则”能把它维系起来？他担心博物馆建筑和设备会压倒整个计划，拒绝把人类学做成考古学、收藏活动与其他事务的 *mixtum compositum*，要求一种“明确的社会科学”，敦促莱顿“社会学化”，并把活的语言与活的文化联系在一起。他对赫伊津哈本人的判断也同样明确：“你在历史学中的方法，在我看来正是一种真正的人类学方法，因此如果你哪怕只是承担教学上的一部分，也会极有价值。”[^14]

Thirteen days later, on 12 August, Huizinga answered by conceding the ‘weakest spots in our scheme’. He admitted that the memorandum contained ‘a bit of window-dressing, or rather of a draft on the future’. The local mechanism, as he described it, was the reorganization of the Ethnographical Museum and closer contact with the Eastern institutes, through which a centre might emerge; making the movement toward sociology more explicit would scare ‘our own men’, though he personally welcomed that direction. Leiden’s leading *adat* scholar was ‘no anthropologist proper’. ‘I myself am only a benevolent outsider.’[^1]

十三天之后，8月12日，赫伊津哈回信承认“我们方案中最薄弱的地方”。他承认备忘录中有“一点橱窗装饰，或者更准确地说，是对未来的一份草案”。按他的描述，莱顿当地真正可行的机制是民族学博物馆的重组，以及它与东方研究机构更紧密的联系；一个中心或许可以由此逐渐形成。若把向社会学移动的方向写得更明确，会吓到“我们自己的人”，尽管赫伊津哈本人欢迎这种方向。莱顿最重要的 *adat* 学者“算不上真正的人类学家”。“我自己不过是一个善意的局外人。”[^1]

The immediate point is institutional. Malinowski could ask Huizinga to play a teaching part while still treating leadership and programme as unsolved disciplinary questions. In Leiden, proximity became a question of who could define and lead the field. Chimisso’s account of the interwar dispute over ‘primitive mentality’ offers a useful comparison: disputes over an object could become disputes over which discipline was competent to speak for it (Chimisso, 2000). In Leiden that question was attached to a proposed centre, its personnel and its programme.

这里最直接的问题是制度性的。马林诺夫斯基可以请赫伊津哈承担教学角色，同时仍把领导权和研究纲领视为尚未解决的学科问题。在莱顿，接近人类学最终变成了谁有权界定并领导这个领域的问题。Chimisso 对战间期“primitive mentality”争论的研究提供了一个有用的比较：围绕某个研究对象的争论，可以同时变成哪一个学科有资格代表它发言的争论（Chimisso, 2000）。在莱顿，这个问题被直接系在一个拟建中心、它的人员与它的研究纲领之上。

A little over a month later, on 15 September, Huizinga wrote, ‘I am sending you a volume just pubished’. Nineteen days later, on 4 October, Malinowski thanked him for the new *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben*, already in use by a pupil working on the couvade, and added that with enough time and a dictionary he could read Dutch. The same letter enclosed a review of Huizinga’s book and said that Malinowski was taking Huizinga’s 12 August institute letter to Paris, where he would raise Leiden with Van Sickle if necessary.[^15]

一个多月以后，9月15日，赫伊津哈写道：“我寄给你一本刚刚出版的书。”十九天之后，10月4日，马林诺夫斯基感谢他寄来的新书 *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben*；这本书已经被一名研究 couvade 的学生使用。他还说，只要给他足够时间和一本词典，他可以读荷兰语。同一封信中还附有一篇关于赫伊津哈著作的评论，并说明马林诺夫斯基正把赫伊津哈8月12日关于研究所的信带往巴黎；如果有必要，他会在那里向 Van Sickle 再谈莱顿计划。[^15]

## Coda: 1932–1933 / 尾声：1932–1933

By 13 November 1932, thirteen months after the October 1931 letter, the exchange was still active: Malinowski thanked Huizinga for sending his article on the relation of the poetic and the theological in Alanus de Insulis; their letters continued to mix scholarly publications with family and academic news. Eleven weeks later, on 30 January 1933, Malinowski told Huizinga that he underestimated his Dutch, said he was willing to write for the Dutch periodical *De Gids*, and even contemplated beginning in Dutch and having Huizinga correct his style.[^18] Their reciprocal reading had become more intimate linguistically before Huizinga marked its disciplinary limit again.

到1932年11月13日，距离1931年10月那封信已经十三个月，两人的往来仍然活跃：马林诺夫斯基感谢赫伊津哈寄来讨论 Alanus de Insulis 中诗性与神学关系的文章；他们的通信继续把学术出版物与家庭、学术界消息混在一起。十一周之后，1933年1月30日，马林诺夫斯基告诉赫伊津哈，他低估了自己的荷兰语能力；他愿意为荷兰期刊 *De Gids* 写文章，甚至考虑先用荷兰语起草，再请赫伊津哈修改文体。[^18] 在赫伊津哈再次明确标出学科界限之前，两人之间相互阅读的关系甚至在语言层面变得更加亲近。

Nearly four months later, on 23 May 1933, Huizinga repeated the distinction when Malinowski stood for the Royal Netherlands Academy: ‘Being no anthropologist I refrained from taking a leading part in your candidature’, though he signed it.[^16] Later that year, in his rectoral address *Over de grenzen van spel en ernst in de cultuur*, Huizinga moved from the Trobriand Kula exchange—circulation, prestige, trust, generosity—to Mauss’s potlatch and then to Philip the Good’s 1454 Burgundian *Vœu du Faisan*, ‘not so very far’ from a potlatch. Semitic and Sanskrit play vocabulary returned; Genoa and Antwerp brought wagering and calculation (Huizinga, 1933). The comparison remained heterogeneous: Kula remained Kula, Burgundy remained Burgundy, and the old philologist remained inside the historian.

将近四个月之后，1933年5月23日，当马林诺夫斯基竞选荷兰皇家科学院席位时，赫伊津哈再次重复了这一区分：“因为我不是人类学家，所以我没有在你的候选提名中承担领导作用”，尽管他仍然署了名。[^16] 同年稍晚，在校长演说 *Over de grenzen van spel en ernst in de cultuur* 中，赫伊津哈从特罗布里恩的 Kula 交换——流通、声望、信任、慷慨——转向莫斯的 potlatch，再转到好人菲利普1454年的勃艮第 *Vœu du Faisan*，称其“离 potlatch 并没有那么远”。闪米特语与梵语中的游戏词汇重新出现；热那亚与安特卫普则带入赌博与计算（Huizinga, 1933）。这个比较仍然保持异质性：Kula 还是 Kula，勃艮第还是勃艮第，而那个老语文学家仍然留在历史学家之中。

The verb ‘refrained’ records an action as much as a limit. Histories of intellectual exchange readily follow successful uptake—what was borrowed, adopted, transmitted or institutionalized—but this correspondence also preserves choices not to complete a transfer. Malinowski postponed his promised Rockefeller intervention after reading the Leiden memorandum; Huizinga helped assemble the centre while declining to claim the anthropological leadership it lacked, and in 1933 supported Malinowski’s candidature while deliberately not taking a leading part. Movement and self-limitation belonged to the same exchange.

“refrained（克制／不参与）”这个动词记录的是一个行动，同时也是一个界限。思想交流史很容易追踪成功的吸收——什么被借用、采纳、传播或制度化——但这批通信同样保存了那些选择不把转移完成的行动。马林诺夫斯基读完莱顿备忘录后，推迟了原先答应的洛克菲勒介入；赫伊津哈参与组织中心，却没有声称承担其中缺失的人类学领导权；1933年，他支持马林诺夫斯基的候选资格，却有意不承担领导角色。移动与自我限制属于同一场交换。

In the 1933 address itself, Huizinga frames comparison as a boundary problem. After Kula and potlatch he asks where seriousness ends and play begins, then turns to wagering and calculation in Genoa and Antwerp (Huizinga, 1933). Ethnology, medieval court culture, philology and economic history remain in the same comparison; play is another question moving among them.

在1933年的演说本身中，赫伊津哈把比较构造成一个边界问题。在 Kula 与 potlatch 之后，他追问严肃在哪里终止、游戏在哪里开始，随后又转向热那亚与安特卫普的赌博和计算（Huizinga, 1933）。民族学、中世纪宫廷文化、语文学与经济史仍然处在同一个比较之中；“游戏”成为在这些领域之间移动的另一个问题。

The contrast between 1929 and 1933 is visible in the work done by different terms and relations. In 1929 ‘the stage of savage life’ could still organize a course.[^7] In 1933 circulation, obligation, prestige, rivalry and play carried the striking comparison (Huizinga, 1933). *Primitive* remained in use while more specific relations bore more of the proof.

1929与1933之间的差异，可以从不同词语和关系实际承担了多少工作中看出来。1929年，“野蛮生活的阶段”仍然可以组织一门课程。[^7] 到1933年，真正承担那次醒目比较的，已经是流通、义务、声望、竞争和游戏（Huizinga, 1933）。*Primitive* 仍然在使用，但越来越具体的关系承担了越来越多的证明工作。

This is also why the article stops at 1933. From 1934 onward the play-centred line expands into a different corpus, while the political problem of culture belongs to a later body of evidence. Following either line would require another archive. The direct Huizinga–Malinowski exchange up to 1933 shows continued recombination.

这也是本文停在1933年的原因。从1934年开始，以游戏为中心的线索进入另一组材料，而文化的政治问题则属于更晚的一套证据。继续追踪其中任何一条线索，都需要另一套档案。到1933年为止，赫伊津哈—马林诺夫斯基的直接往来能够显示的是持续的重新组合。

The later twentieth-century encounter between anthropology and social history offers a useful contrast. Anthropological exemplars could remake historians’ questions and accounts of social change (Foks, 2024). The exchange reconstructed here belongs to an earlier, less settled configuration. Anthropology could already reorganize historical questions without transferring disciplinary authority. The later anthropological turn can make this earlier exchange look like a precursor. The archive points instead to a different configuration: by 1933 questions could move far across the boundary while disciplinary authority remained uneven.

二十世纪后期人类学与社会史之间的相遇提供了一个有用的对照。人类学范例后来可以重塑历史学家的问题以及他们关于社会变迁的叙述（Foks, 2024）。本文重建的往来属于更早、也更不稳定的配置。人类学已经能够重组历史问题，却不必因此把学科权威一并转移。后来的“人类学转向”很容易把这段较早的往来看成一个前驱。档案指向的却是另一种结构：到1933年，问题已经可以跨越很远的学科边界，而学科权威仍然分布不均。

Leiden had already given this waning an institutional form. Shared questions could move between anthropology and history; authority over their comparison did not move so easily. ‘Benevolent outsider’ was Huizinga’s name for that position. The word *primitive* remained. More and more of the proof stood outside it.

莱顿已经给这种 *waning* 一个制度形式。共同的问题可以在人类学与历史学之间移动；但对这些比较拥有权威，却没有那么容易一起移动。“善意的局外人”是赫伊津哈给这种位置起的名字。*Primitive* 这个词仍然存在。越来越多的证明工作，却站到了这个词之外。
