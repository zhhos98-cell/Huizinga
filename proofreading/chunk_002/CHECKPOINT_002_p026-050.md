## High-value discovery in this range

The p25 opening, now separately text-checked, controls interpretation of the Florence block. Huizinga explicitly calls the development of Florence to the rise of the Medici `een bloedige gesch.` and places `partijstrijd en hebzucht` beside the city's `prachtige bloei`. Pages 26–47 then execute that programme rather than abandoning it: political institutions, class/guild structure, finance, faction, palazzi, churches, poetry, sculpture, painting, humanism, and Medici culture are interleaved in one continuous analytical sequence.

That matters for later corpus-level indexing. The correct tag for this unit is not simply `Florence / Renaissance art`; it is closer to:

`Florence — political/social conflict + urban institutions + economic structure + built form + literary/visual culture`.
