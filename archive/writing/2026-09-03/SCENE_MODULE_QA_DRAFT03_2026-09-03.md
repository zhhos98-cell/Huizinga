`Van Sickle briefs Malinowski → Malinowski expresses unreserved enthusiasm with little detail → memorandum arrives → Malinowski audits → unsolicited intervention postponed pending Huizinga’s answer`.

This makes reading itself historically consequential.

# Current preferred article logic

> The relationship became historically revealing not because one man converted the other, but because each new object placed a heavier institutional burden on an already reciprocal practice of reading. Books could cross the boundary easily; manuscripts could be judged across it; an institute forced the pair to say who possessed the authority to define anthropology; the traffic continued even after that limit was named.

Shortest version:

> **Books crossed. Questions crossed. Authority did not automatically cross with them.**
