# Nationaal Archief 2.21.271 inv. 836 — C.J.J.G. Vosmaer / Saint Lucas Society

_Status: primary archival lead, synced 2026-09-03._

## Why this file matters

Nationaal Archief, Collectie 548 Vosmaer (access 2.21.271), contains the following item:

**inv. 836 — `Stukken betreffende de werkzaamheden van Carel J.J.G. Vosmaer als directeur van de "St. Lucas Society, archief voor Genealogiese Documentatie" te Leiden.` 1947 en z.j., 1 omslag.**

This is currently the most direct archival unit for the post-1945 Huizinga-library dispersal problem because it concerns **C.J.J.G. Vosmaer (1907–1986)** in his capacity as director of Saint Lucas Society, immediately after Johan Huizinga's death and in the period when the majority of Huizinga's private books passed to Vosmaer.

Source:
- Nationaal Archief finding aid, 2.21.271: https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/archief/2.21.271/download/pdf

Nationaal Archief offers **Scanning on Demand** for archival items that are not yet digitized. Its current tariff page states that a user can order scans from home via the `Bestel scans` control at the relevant inventarisnummer. The charge is **EUR 16.75 per inventarisnummer** as search/research cost; the order includes **up to 250 scans**, digitally delivered. Standard scans within that allowance are otherwise charged EUR 0.00.

Thus inv. 836 is not a compulsory in-person archive visit. If the `Bestel scans` control is enabled for this public item, the whole folder can potentially be obtained remotely for EUR 16.75 provided it does not exceed 250 scans and has no material restriction.

## What to inspect if scans are obtained

Search the complete folder, not only obvious Huizinga references, for:

- `Huizinga`, `Johan Huizinga`, family names and addresses;
- `Malinowski`, `Argonauts`, `Crime and Custom`, `Sex and Repression`;
- stock lists, accession notes, purchase lists, invoices and receipts;
- catalogue drafts / printer's copy / correspondence concerning **Catalogue 3, Wetenschappelijke adversaria**;
- wording about `opdrachten`, `brieven`, `ex libris`, `uit bibliotheek`, `nalatenschap`;
- client correspondence and mailing lists;
- Rapenburg 83, Wyttenbachweg 43, Saint Lucas / St. Lucas, *Bibliopolis*, *Rapiarijs*;
- handwritten stock numbers that could correlate with the small ink numbers found in surviving C.J.J.G. Vosmaer books.

## Evidential caution

The finding-aid title alone does not tell us what kinds of documents the folder contains. It may be administrative rather than bookselling material. No stock list, invoice, Huizinga reference or Catalogue 3 draft should be asserted until the folder itself is inspected.

## Priority

**Tier 1 / direct route.** This item outranks the 1986 Van Stockum *Bibliotheca Vosmaeriana* sale because the latter primarily concerns the inherited nineteenth-century family library of Carel Vosmaer (1826–1888), whereas inv. 836 explicitly concerns C.J.J.G.'s own postwar Saint Lucas activities.
