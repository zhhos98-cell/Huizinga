# F.M. / S.M. Scholten-Huizinga initials anomaly — resolution note (2026-09-04)

_Status: identity-resolution note. Strong convergence, but one initials discrepancy remains in the printed 1994 source._

## 1. Secure/near-secure anchors

Anton van der Lem identifies Huizinga's **oldest granddaughter** as **Mrs F.M. Scholten-Huizinga at Sterksel**, in whose possession Huizinga's blue-cloth set of Robert Fruin's *Verspreide Geschriften* then remained. Leiden UBL's recovered-books list later records **F.M. Scholten-Huizinga** as donor of HUIZIN 2032 (Pirenne) and HUIZIN 2039 (Fruin, 11 vols.).

Sources:
- Anton van der Lem, *Het Eeuwige verbeeld in een afgehaald bed*, note 51: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/lem_002eeuw01_01/lem_002eeuw01_01_0004.php
- Huizinga Online / UBL recovered-books list: https://huizinga-online.nl/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Lijst-van-teruggevonden-boeken.pdf

## 2. Full-name control from the edited correspondence

The editors of *Briefwisseling* III explicitly identify **Franziska Margaretha Huizinga** in a biographical annotation, referring back to *Briefwisseling* II [856]. This is an important control because the full name appears in the scholarly Huizinga correspondence apparatus, independently of modern genealogy websites.

Source:
- *Briefwisseling* III, editorial note naming `Franziska Margaretha Huizinga`: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2a/Briefwisseling%2C_Deel_III_1934-1945%2C_DigUBL.pdf

## 3. Convergence with the Sterksel / Scholten identity

Multiple modern genealogical compilations identify **Franziska Margaretha `Fransje` Huizinga**, born 1929 at Soekaboemi/Sukabumi, as a daughter of **Leonhard Huizinga** and Paula Rosa Kestler, later married to **Ludwig Jan Herman Scholten**, and resident/dying at Sterksel. These compilations vary in exact death date and are not primary evidence, so they are used only as identity-convergence aids.

The combination is highly specific:

`Franziska Margaretha Huizinga` -> initials **F.M.** -> daughter of **Leonhard** -> married **Scholten** -> **Sterksel**.

This matches Van der Lem's `F.M. Scholten-Huizinga te Sterksel` exactly at the level of initials, surname, locality and family branch.

Examples:
- Genealogie Online, Franziska Margaretha `Fransje` Huizinga: https://www.genealogieonline.nl/en/genealogie-koek/I1287.php
- Genealogie Online, alternate compilation: https://www.genealogieonline.nl/van-adiks-tot-zuiderhoudt/I3271.php

## 4. The 1994 printed `S.M.` anomaly

The *Biografie Bulletin* article `Grootvader Huizinga: Herinneringen van een kleindochter` is printed under **S.M. Scholten-Huizinga**. The speaker says:

- she is Huizinga's **oldest grandchild**;
- **Leonhard is her father**;
- she spent time with Huizinga at Leiden as a child.

At the preceding biography presentation, Anton van der Lem likewise addresses `mevrouw Scholten-Huizinga` as Huizinga's **oldest granddaughter** and one of the two granddaughters with personal memories of him.

Sources:
- `Grootvader Huizinga`, *Biografie Bulletin* 4 (1994): https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_bio001199401_01/_bio001199401_01_0006.php
- Anton van der Lem, `Bij de biografie van Johan Huizinga`: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_bio001199401_01/_bio001199401_01_0005.php

## 5. Working resolution

The evidence strongly indicates that the printed **S.M. Scholten-Huizinga** and Van der Lem's **F.M. Scholten-Huizinga** refer to the same oldest granddaughter, very likely **Franziska Margaretha (`Fransje`) Huizinga**, daughter of Leonhard, later Scholten-Huizinga of Sterksel.

However, because the 1994 article itself prints `S.M.` and no primary civil/genealogical record has yet been inspected to explain that initial, the repository should preserve the discrepancy explicitly rather than silently emend it.

Recommended citation form in research notes:

> F.M. Scholten-Huizinga (very likely Franziska Margaretha `Fransje` Huizinga, Leonhard's daughter; the 1994 *Biografie Bulletin* prints the anomalous initials `S.M.` for what appears to be the same eldest granddaughter).

## 6. Provenance consequence

This materially strengthens the Leonhard family-retention branch. We now have a probable named chain:

`Johan Huizinga -> Leonhard branch -> Franziska Margaretha (F.M.) Scholten-Huizinga, Sterksel -> UBL HUIZIN 2032 / 2039`

The exact transfer step `Johan -> Leonhard -> Franziska` is still not documented item-by-item. The books may have passed directly to the granddaughter or through another family intermediary. But the branch identity itself is no longer anonymous.
