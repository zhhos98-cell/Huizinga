# Leiden UBL BA2 route for Huizinga / Saint Lucas provenance — 2026-09-04

_Status: focused archival route note. Supplements `huizinga_library_provenance_update_2026-09-04.md`._

## 1. The relevant Leiden University Library institutional archive is now identified

Leiden University Libraries' current overview of Special Collections archives explicitly lists:

- **Archieven Universiteit Leiden: Universiteitsbibliotheek, (1800)1924–1977(2007)**
- **Signatuur: BA2**
- **Collection guide: ubl100**
- a **PDF inventory/access aid** attached through the collection guide.

This is the institutional archive needed for the 1946–1952 Saint Lucas/Vosmaer search. It covers the entire postwar window in which Huizinga's books were divided, C.J.J.G. Vosmaer's Saint Lucas file is dated 1947+n.d., and *Bibliopolis* advertised the postwar Catalogue 3 around 1948.

The online collection-guide host is not currently yielding its attached BA2 inventory to ordinary indexed-web retrieval, so the next action is to obtain/open the `ubl100` BA2 PDF itself rather than infer inventory numbers from secondary references.

## 2. BA2 uses item-level archival signatures and is actively citable

A 2026 Leiden Medievalists article cites a concrete item in this same archive as **BA2 S373.1**, alongside earlier BA1 records. This does not identify the Saint Lucas series, but it confirms that BA2 is an item-level working archive rather than only a high-level institutional description.

Operationally, once the BA2 inventory is obtained, search its headings/index for:

- `aankoop`, `aankopen`, `aanwinsten`, `acquisitie`;
- `boekhandel`, `boekhandelaren`, `antiquariaat`, `antiquaren`;
- `rekeningen`, `facturen`, `leveranciers`;
- `correspondentie` and `ingekomen/uitgaande stukken`;
- `Kessen`, `Vosmaer`, `Saint Lucas`, `St. Lucas`, `Rapenburg`;
- `catalogi`, `circulaires`, `aanbiedingen`.

## 3. Why BA2 is unusually promising: Saint Lucas was a major UBL supplier/service partner

J.F. Heijbroek states that one of the Saint Lucas Society's most important customers was Leiden University Library. Vosmaer performed microfilming, reproduction, stencilling and publishing work for the library. This relationship means that BA2 may preserve more than purchases: supplier correspondence, invoices, circulars or received catalogues could survive even if no Huizinga-origin volume was bought by UBL at the time.

The target is therefore not merely `did UBL buy a Huizinga book?` but also `what documentary trace did the institution retain of Saint Lucas as a vendor/service provider during 1947–49?`

Source:
- J.F. Heijbroek, `De Saint Lucas Society`, *De Boekenwereld* 34.4 (2018): https://www.bibliotheek.universiteitleiden.nl/binaries/content/assets/ul2ub/bijzondere-collecties/de-boekenwereld/de-boekenwereld-34_4-2018.pdf

## 4. Known recovered Huizinga books provide control specimens for provenance encoding

Anton van der Lem records two especially useful market-recovery examples:

- his predecessor as Leiden UBL conservator, **Dr. R. Breugelmans**, acquired Huizinga's copies of P.S. Allen's edition of Erasmus's letters;
- another colleague found Huizinga's copy of Thomas à Kempis, *De Imitatione Christi*, at an antiquarian bookseller in Dordrecht.

These are valuable as **control specimens**. If their accession or acquisition records can be identified in BA2 or successor administrative files, they can show how UBL historically recorded Huizinga provenance, vendor identity, price, accession number and date. That vocabulary/record structure can then be reused for the Malinowski search.

Source:
- Anton van der Lem, `Een bibliotheek als wederopstanding`: https://huizinga-online.nl/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Een-bibliotheek-als-wederopstanding.pdf

## 5. The 2018 HUIZIN list gives a second control specimen: HUIZIN 2036

The public recovered-books list (last updated 4 December 2018) records:

- **HUIZIN 2036** — A.J. Wensinck, *De Nederlandsch-Hervormde Kerk en de Gemeente van Christus* (Rotterdam, [1938]); inscription `In opdracht v.d. S.` and **ex libris C.J.J.G. Vosmaer**; gift of Prof. W.E. Krul.

A text search of this 2018 list for `Vosmaer` yields this explicit entry. It is currently the clearest published control object for the chain `Huizinga provenance + C.J.J.G. Vosmaer bookplate`.

The same list records **HUIZIN 2007**, Thomas à Kempis, *De imitatione Christi libri IV*, G. de Gregor ed. (Leipzig, 1840), with Huizinga's dated ownership inscription `Dr. J. Huizinga : 7.XII.'98`, gift of Hans van der Velden. It may or may not be the Dordrecht-antiquarian copy mentioned by Van der Lem; that identity should not be assumed without acquisition documentation.

Source:
- UBL/Huizinga Online, `Overzicht van de boeken uit het bezit van prof. dr. J. Huizinga, thans aanwezig in de UB Leiden` (last updated 4 Dec. 2018): https://huizinga-online.nl/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Lijst-van-teruggevonden-boeken.pdf

## 6. Immediate archival test sequence

1. Obtain/open the **ubl100 BA2 inventory PDF**.
2. Identify the BA2 series covering **1946–1952 acquisitions, supplier correspondence, invoices and received catalogues**.
3. Run the names/terms `Vosmaer`, `Saint Lucas`, `Rapenburg 83`, `Bibliopolis`, `Wetenschappelijke adversaria`, `catalogus 3` against the inventory and, where possible, item descriptions.
4. Locate the accession trail for the **Breugelmans / P.S. Allen Erasmus** acquisition and the **Thomas à Kempis** recovery as control cases.
5. Extract record-format clues: vendor wording, accession numbering, date, price, provenance notes and any recurring supplier file.
6. Apply the same record-format clues to `Malinowski`, `Crime and Custom`, `Sex and Repression`, `Argonauts` and possible presentation-copy descriptors.
7. In parallel, ask whether UBL retained **received antiquarian catalogues/circulars** from Saint Lucas; Catalogue 3 might survive as administrative ephemera rather than a separately catalogued book-trade item.

## 7. Evidential boundary

At this stage BA2 is a **confirmed archive target**, not yet a confirmed Saint Lucas hit. No BA2 inventory number for Vosmaer/Saint Lucas has been identified online. The value of this pass is that the institutional route is now reduced from `search Leiden records` to the concrete archive **BA2 / collection guide ubl100 / attached PDF inventory**, with two known Huizinga recovery cases available as controls.
