# Van Stockum auction working archive (HGA 1500-01): collateral route

_Status: active but demoted after identity correction, 2026-09-03._

## Identity correction

The Van Stockum sale of 3–5 December 1986 is documented as the sale of the **last part of the `Bibliotheca Vosmaeriana`**, but the relevant provenance literature is discussing the inherited collection of **Carel Vosmaer (1826–1888)**. It must not be automatically identified with the twentieth-century personal or Saint Lucas stock of his grandson **C.J.J.G. Vosmaer (1907–1986)**, who acquired much of Johan Huizinga's library after 1945.

Accordingly, HGA 1500-01 is retained as a potentially useful **collateral** source, not the primary Huizinga-Malinowski route.

## Why HGA 1500-01 remains useful

A 2016 interview with Van Stockum director Peter Pruimers states that the post-1906 portion of the firm's archive was transferred to the Haags Gemeentearchief. The archive includes auction catalogues annotated per lot with the **consignor (`inbrenger`), buyer (`koper`) and price (`prijs`)**. If any C.J.J.G./Saint Lucas material was mixed into the December 1986 family consignment, such a working copy could reveal it.

Source:
- J.F. Heijbroek and Nick ter Wal, interview with Peter Pruimers, *De Boekenwereld* 32 (2016): https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_boe022201601_01/_boe022201601_01_0054.php

## Target event

Contemporary auction agendas record a Van Stockum book sale at Prinsegracht 15, The Hague, **3–5 December 1986**, with viewing 28–30 November. A later *Van Gogh Museum Journal* note identifies the sale as the final dispersal of the older family *Bibliotheca Vosmaeriana*.

Sources:
- *De Boekenwereld* 3 (1986–1987), auction agenda: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_boe022198601_01/_boe022198601_01_0021.php
- *Van Gogh Museum Journal* 1996: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_van012199601_01/_van012199601_01.pdf

The exact item-level inventarisnummer for the 3–5 Dec. 1986 working catalogue inside 1500-01 has not yet been recovered.

## Scan-on-request possibility

Haags Gemeentearchief offers `Scannen op verzoek` for eligible archival units. An exact archive/item number is required. If the December 1986 working catalogue is eligible once its item number is known, it may be obtainable remotely.

## Proper evidential use

The HGA working catalogue would become relevant to Huizinga only if it supplies one of the following:

- a Malinowski title matching Huizinga's three copies;
- an explicit C.J.J.G. / Saint Lucas / Huizinga consignor note;
- another recognizable post-1945 C.J.J.G. stock marker mixed into the sale.

Without such evidence, the sale belongs to the older Vosmaer family-library history and should not be used to infer Huizinga provenance.

## Primary route now elsewhere

For the three Huizinga-Malinowski books, the stronger archival route is now:

1. **Nationaal Archief 2.21.271 inv. 836** — C.J.J.G. Vosmaer's Saint Lucas Society activities, 1947+n.d., 1 omslag.
2. Saint Lucas Catalogue 3, *Wetenschappelijke adversaria*.
3. Object-level searches for C.J.J.G.'s heraldic ex libris and small ink inventory numbers on Malinowski editions.

HGA 1500-01 should remain in the research graph as a well-defined fallback, but not consume the main search effort until a direct C.J.J.G./Huizinga connection to the 1986 consignment appears.
