# Van Stockum auction working archive (HGA 1500-01)

_Status: active provenance lead; first synced 2026-09-03._

## Why this archive matters

The strongest current route for tracing the December 1986 dispersal of the *Bibliotheca Vosmaeriana* is no longer merely the public Van Stockum sale catalogue. A 2016 interview with Van Stockum director Peter Pruimers states that in spring 2016 he transferred the post-1906 portion of the firm's archive to the Haags Gemeentearchief. The interview describes the material as providing a full view of the firm's twentieth-century operations and, crucially, as containing **auction catalogues annotated per lot with the consignor (`inbrenger`), buyer (`koper`), and price (`prijs`)**.

Source:
- J.F. Heijbroek and Nick ter Wal, interview with Peter Pruimers, *De Boekenwereld* 32 (2016): https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_boe022201601_01/_boe022201601_01_0054.php

This is potentially decisive for the Huizinga-Malinowski provenance search. The public catalogue can establish that a Malinowski title was offered, but the Van Stockum working copy could potentially establish all three sides of the transaction: the estate/consignor, the lot, and the post-sale buyer.

## Target event

Independent evidence identifies the relevant estate sale as:

**Van Stockum, The Hague, 3–5 December 1986.**

A contemporary *De Boekenwereld* agenda records the Van Stockum sale on those dates. A later *Van Gogh Museum Journal* provenance note states that the **last part of the `Bibliotheca Vosmaeriana`** was sold at Van Stockum in The Hague on 3–5 December 1986.

Sources:
- *De Boekenwereld* 3 (1986–1987), auction agenda: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_boe022198601_01/_boe022198601_01_0021.php
- *Van Gogh Museum Journal* 1996, provenance note on the Vosmaer collection: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_van012199601_01/_van012199601_01.pdf

The immediate archival target is therefore the **working/annotated Van Stockum catalogue for the 3–5 December 1986 sale** inside access 1500-01. Its exact item-level `inventarisnummer` has not yet been recovered from the broken public finding aid.

## Scan-on-request possibility

Haags Gemeentearchief currently offers a free `Scannen op verzoek` service for eligible archival units. The workflow requires an exact archive/item number; requests are limited to one archival item per week and, when eligible, the resulting scans are delivered by email link. Therefore recovering the exact inventarisnummer inside 1500-01 could turn this into a remote-access source rather than requiring an in-person visit.

Source:
- Haags Gemeentearchief, `Scannen op verzoek` instructions.

Eligibility for the 1986 working catalogue has not yet been established; the service should be treated as a concrete possibility, not a guarantee.

## Important pre-1986 branch: Van Gendt

The 1996 *Van Gogh Museum Journal* note also states that **Van Gendt Book Auctions sold portions of the Vosmaer collection during the 1980s**, while the December 1986 Van Stockum sale represented only the last part. The working dispersal model must therefore include an earlier 1980s Van Gendt branch.

Consequently, failure to find one or all of the three Malinowski titles in the December 1986 Van Stockum catalogue would not imply family retention. They may already have left the Vosmaer collection through Van Gendt or another earlier sale.

## Backup catalogue repositories

Two institutional routes remain important if the HGA working copy cannot immediately be isolated:

1. The former KVB / Bibliotheek van de Vereeniging book-trade collections (now associated with Allard Pierson) are documented as preserving auction catalogues with buyer annotations.
2. The Koninklijke Bibliotheek holds a very large collection of Dutch auction and book-trade catalogues; its collection description notes that some catalogues contain handwritten buyer annotations, price information, or printed results.

These are backup paths until an exact 3–5 December 1986 holding is identified.

## Evidence status

**Confirmed:**
- The relevant public sale occurred at Van Stockum, The Hague, 3–5 Dec. 1986.
- It is described as the sale of the last part of the *Bibliotheca Vosmaeriana*.
- Van Stockum's twentieth-century archive was transferred to HGA in 2016.
- Pruimers described that archive as containing auction catalogues annotated per lot with consignor, buyer and price.
- HGA 0770-01 explicitly cross-references access 1500-01 `Van Stockum's Veilingen`.

**Not yet confirmed:**
- The exact item number for the 3–5 Dec. 1986 working catalogue within 1500-01.
- That this particular catalogue survives in annotated form, although the collection-level description makes this a strong hypothesis.
- That any of Huizinga's three Malinowski books appeared in the sale.
