## Controlled passage

Discussing Spengler's *Preussentum und Sozialismus* (1921), Huizinga says the work reveals the weakness of Spengler's method. He calls it a political doctrine based on a `historisch-ethnografische tegenstelling`, specifically the opposition between English and Prussian character. He then describes the method:

`De schrijver poneert een tegenstelling, en brengt vervolgens alles, wat hem te pas komt, onder de suggestieve werking van dat contrast.`

The point is not simply that Spengler is political. Huizinga identifies a methodological operation:

1. posit a historical–ethnographic contrast;
2. allow the contrast to become suggestive/organizing;
3. subsume convenient particulars beneath it;
4. derive a political doctrine through that structure.
