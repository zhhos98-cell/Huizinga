# Huizinga's three Malinowski books — provenance closure

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: DORMANT / FROZEN PROVENANCE BRANCH

This note replaces a large active-surface cluster of physical-copy, catalogue, estate, Saint Lucas, Vosmaer, KB and antiquarian-route searches. Detailed provenance is preserved under `archive/research_provenance/malinowski_books/`.

## What is secure

Huizinga's 12 December 1926 letter establishes receipt of three Malinowski books. The *Briefwisseling* apparatus identifies them as *Crime and Custom in Savage Society*, *Sex and Repression in Savage Society*, and *Argonauts of the Western Pacific*. That correspondence evidence is sufficient for the article's book-transaction chain; recovery of the physical copies is not required to narrate receipt, reading choice, or the later Kula trajectory.

No surviving copy of the three targets is presently proven to be Johan Huizinga's own copy by presentation inscription, ownership hand, documented chain of custody, or another independent copy-level signal.

The public Leiden `HUIZIN` reconstruction dated 4 December 2018 contains no match for Malinowski, *Argonauts*, *Crime and Custom*, or *Sex and Repression*. This negative is bounded to that public reconstruction, not all Leiden holdings or later identifications.

A Leiden University Libraries copy of *Sex and Repression in Savage Society* is callable at Van Steenis Depot, shelfmark `2459 D 9` (MMS `990018821830302711`). Its public record does not establish Huizinga provenance. It is the strongest concrete object to inspect if the branch is deliberately reopened.

## What the detailed work established without closing the object chain

The archived searches reconstructed possible postwar dispersal systems: Saint Lucas / C.J.J.G. Vosmaer; Leiden institutional acquisitions and later HUIZIN reconstruction; an unidentified Koninklijke Bibliotheek batch of roughly fifty Huizinga books donated by Auguste Huizinga-Schölvinck in 1965; family-retention / donor controls; and later catalogue, auction and provenance-image routes. None tied one of the target books securely to Huizinga.

Vosmaer provenance is not sufficient by itself: multiple ex-libris types are documented and require a second independent Huizinga signal before a copy can be attributed.

## Reopen rule

Do not restart generic title searching, publisher history, Saint Lucas expansion, Vosmaer family tracing, auction searching, or broad catalogue reconstruction.

Reopen only if one of these appears:

1. a target copy with a plausible Malinowski presentation inscription or Johan Huizinga ownership hand;
2. a catalogue / accession / donor record explicitly naming a target edition and linking it to Huizinga or his dispersal chain;
3. a stock or inventory number independently connected both to a target title and Huizinga provenance;
4. direct archival dispatch evidence changing the state or identity of one of the December 1926 books.

If reopened, test Leiden `2459 D 9` first, then exact-title UBL donor/accession records and the KB 1965 batch.
