# 1926 methodological caution / 1931 institutional classification tension control — 2026-09-05

Purpose: prevent Draft 04 from turning the waning of `primitive` into a smooth retreat from classificatory language.

## 1926 control

The article already source-locks Huizinga's 7 April 1926 theses, `Taak en termen der beschavingsgeschiedenis` (*Verslag van de algemeene vergadering der leden van het Historisch Genootschap*, published 1927, pp. 5–7).

Secure points already in Draft 04:

- Huizinga attacked `evolutie` as a general historical principle;
- he defined cultural history as a social morphology concerned with relations connecting people;
- he placed ethnology among special cultural sciences practising such morphology;
- he warned that general culture-historical terms could acquire a `mythological element` and undergo inflation;
- period names claiming to state an era's essence could `prejudge and mislead`.

This establishes methodological suspicion toward allowing a general term or temporal sequence to settle the historical argument in advance.

## 1931 institutional control

The Leiden Centre packet is separately controlled:

- 17 July: Huizinga's paper had already circulated locally, including to Van Eerde, and been revised; Snouck Hurgronje was proposed as another reader.
- 19 July: Huizinga sent Malinowski `a paper which I made up` and asked for judgment / possible Rockefeller help.
- the packet comprised Huizinga's seven-page signed memorandum plus A. W. Nieuwenhuis's two-page signed annex.
- the memorandum used developmental/civilizational classifications including `backward stage`, Java as `high culture`, and Bali as a Hindu remnant.
- Nieuwenhuis's annex was titled `Ethnographical objects as data for psychological research about primitive or semi-cultured races` and linked objects to inferred mental capacities, developmental position, and practical administrative/economic questions.

The annex is Nieuwenhuis's text. Do not attribute its exact classificatory language or inference chain directly to Huizinga. But Huizinga participated in composing, circulating, revising, and forwarding the packet as an institutional object.

## Historical significance

The 1926 methodological caution did not produce a steady chronological withdrawal from developmental classification. In 1931, within an institutional proposal Huizinga helped assemble and route, classificatory language again performed hard practical work.

This is counterevidence against a teleology:

`METHOD CRITIQUE OF GENERAL TERMS (1926) != CLASSIFICATION DISAPPEARS FROM PRACTICE (1931)`

Instead it supports the article's governing formulation:

`FORM PERSISTS + INFERENTIAL SUFFICIENCY VARIES BY SETTING + EXPLANATORY / ADMINISTRATIVE WORK IS REDISTRIBUTED`.

The 1931 episode is especially useful because the category's force is institutional, not simply conceptual. A label may remain operational in a collaborative administrative/scientific packet even when the historian forwarding it has elsewhere warned against allowing general terms to prejudge historical understanding.

## Article decision

Add at most one sentence after the paragraph reconstructing the Nieuwenhuis chain. Keep responsibility exact:

`Huizinga's 1926 warning about general historical terms had therefore not produced a steady retreat from classification: the annex was Nieuwenhuis's, but Huizinga helped assemble and forward a packet in which developmental labels still did practical work.`

Do not turn this into a character contradiction or accuse Huizinga of personally authoring Nieuwenhuis's psychology/administration chain. The point is variation by intellectual and institutional setting.
