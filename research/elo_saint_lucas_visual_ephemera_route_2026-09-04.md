# Erfgoed Leiden visual-ephemera route for Saint Lucas — 2026-09-04

_Status: confirmed image/ephemera repository route; exact ELO image signatures not yet recovered from public indexing._

## 1. Positive institutional evidence

J.F. Heijbroek's 2019 postscript/article on Leiden antiquarian bookshops reproduces two Saint Lucas objects explicitly credited to **Beeldbank Erfgoed Leiden en Omstreken (ELO)**:

1. a photograph of **The Saint Lucas Society, Rapenburg 83, Leiden**, showing the shopfront/etalages, photographed by Nico van der Horst;
2. a **Saint Lucas Society advertising sheet** printed in `Serigraphie`, described as a cheap multicolour printing method for small and medium print runs up to c. 10,000 copies.

This proves that ELO holds at least one piece of **Saint Lucas commercial printed ephemera**, not merely street photography.

Source:
- J.F. Heijbroek, postscript/article in *De Boekenwereld* 35.4 (2019), p. 69: https://www.bibliotheek.universiteitleiden.nl/binaries/content/assets/ul2ub/bijzondere-collecties/de-boekenwereld/de-boekenwereld-35_4-2019compressed.pdf

## 2. Why this matters for Catalogue 3

Catalogue 3 may have escaped ordinary library cataloguing because Saint Lucas material often circulated as small catalogues, newsletters, advertisements and business ephemera. ELO's confirmed preservation of a Saint Lucas advertising sheet demonstrates a fourth documentary ecology alongside:

- KVB/Allard Pierson book-trade catalogues;
- KB `Verzameling Catalogi`;
- Leiden UBL institutional archive BA2;
- Vosmaer family / production-design material.

The ELO image bank should therefore be searched not only for `Catalogue 3`, but for **visual/ephemeral neighbours** that may share a donor accession or image series.

## 4. Object-fingerprint consequence

A separate 2019 bookseller-label history notes that postwar Saint Lucas advertisements called Rapenburg 83 **`het huis met de blauwe zonneschermen`**. This phrase is potentially useful in newspaper OCR, advertisements and visual cataloguing because it may identify Saint Lucas without the formal business name.

Source:
- `Boekhandelsetiketjes: Saint Lucas` (2019): https://boekhandelsetiketjes.blogspot.com/2019/12/saint-lucas.html

## 5. Immediate archival test

1. Obtain the ELO image-bank record/signature for the `Serigraphie` Saint Lucas advertising sheet reproduced by Heijbroek.
2. Inspect accession/donor metadata and adjacent record numbers.
3. Determine whether the ELO holding is an isolated reproduction or belongs to a folder/collection of Saint Lucas ephemera.
4. Search that same accession group for catalogue covers, price lists, newsletters, correspondence cards, business labels and `Bibliopolis` material.
5. If Catalogue 3 itself is absent, use surviving Saint Lucas print ephemera to reconstruct printers, visual identity, addresses and approximate dating.

## 6. Evidential boundary

- **ELO holds Saint Lucas commercial ephemera:** secure.
