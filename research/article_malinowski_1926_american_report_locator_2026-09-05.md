# Malinowski 1926 American-visit report locator — 2026-09-05

_Status: publication-QA locator closure for Draft 04 [^2]. No change to the article's interpretation of the Harvard encounter._

## Object

The report already used in Draft 04 can now be cited by its full archival title and file locator:

**Bronislaw Malinowski, `Report by Dr. B. Malinowski to the Laura Spelman Rockefeller Memorial, Concerning His Travels in America in 1926, March–August`, Rockefeller Archive Center, Laura Spelman Rockefeller Memorial records, LSRM III, 6/56/599.**

The locator is independently reproduced in later scholarship on Rockefeller philanthropy and British/African anthropology, including Sebastian Esselborn's *Die Afrikaexperten*, which cites the report as `RAC LSRM III 6/56/599`.

## Relation to the article

The report is the source for Malinowski's account of his 1926 American programme and for the statement that discussions similar to his theoretical programme were repeated at a Harvard seminar attended by R.B. Dixon, A. Tozzer, Marcel Mauss and Professor Huizinga.

The existing Draft 04 guardrail remains correct:

- the report establishes Huizinga's presence at a seminar where Malinowski says similar discussions occurred;
- the surviving written report supplies the fuller formulation of Malinowski's programme;
- the report does **not** establish that Huizinga heard the written report verbatim or that every sentence of that programme was delivered at Harvard.

Do not substitute the separate August 1926 SSRC Hanover Conference files for this report. Those are adjacent Rockefeller/SSRC evidence, not the source used for the spring Harvard encounter.

## Article decision

Replace the generic `Bronisław Malinowski, report on his 1926 American visit to the Laura Spelman Rockefeller Memorial` in [^2] with the full report title and `Rockefeller Archive Center, LSRM III, 6/56/599`.

No body prose change is warranted.
