# Fieldwork / historical-warrant wording control — 2026-09-04

Purpose: prevent Draft 04 from implying undocumented repeat field access by Malinowski during 1926–33.

Current wording in the Java/archive comparison says `Malinowski could revisit living relations`. That is stronger than the evidence needed for the argument and may suggest that he could repeatedly return to the Trobriands during the interval.

The secure contrast is evidentiary rather than logistical: Malinowski's published ethnographic authority rested on observation of living social relations, whereas the Huizinga dossiers used in this article show historical claims being worked through notes, cards, copies, photographs, autograph letters and surviving texts.

## Article decision

Replace `Malinowski could revisit living relations` with `Malinowski's published ethnography rested on observation of living relations`. Preserve the rest of the paragraph. This keeps the comparison at the level of warrant rather than biographical access.
