# 1926 Harvard seminar — wording control — 2026-09-04

Purpose: keep the opening America scene within the exact scope of Malinowski's Rockefeller report.

## Secure relation

Malinowski's 1926 report to the Laura Spelman Rockefeller Memorial sets out his theoretical programme, including a section headed / framed as `The functional view of Anthropology`. The report then says that **similar theoretical discussions** were repeated in several American settings, including a Harvard seminar attended by R. B. Dixon, A. Tozzer, Marcel Mauss and Professor Huizinga.

## Boundary

The report does not establish that the Harvard seminar itself bore the title `the functional view of Anthropology`, that Huizinga heard every formulation preserved in the written report, or that the written report itself circulated to Huizinga.

This boundary was already recorded in `archive/writing/2026-09-03/SCENE_MODULE_CALIBRATION_1926_1931_2026-09-03.md`.

## Article decision

Replace the opening wording `his report places Huizinga in a seminar on ‘the functional view of Anthropology’` with the narrower `his report places Huizinga in a Harvard seminar where discussions similar to Malinowski's theoretical programme were repeated`.

The later paragraph that separately describes the report's `functional view` section and then says similar discussions were repeated at Harvard is already correctly calibrated.
