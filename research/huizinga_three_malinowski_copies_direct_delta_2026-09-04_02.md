## 1. Huizinga ownership fingerprint: handwritten name outranks any Huizinga ex libris

Anton van der Lem's 2015 article on Huizinga's ex-librises gives an important search correction. He states that, among the roughly fifty Huizinga books recovered at that point, none contained Huizinga's own ex libris. The practical recovery tool was instead Huizinga's ownership inscription: `J. Huizinga`, in his neat hand, normally in the upper-right corner of the title page or front cover.

The same article warns that an ex libris reading `J. Huizinga` which had begun appearing in Dutch antiquarian stock belonged to Johan's elder brother **Jakob Huizinga (1870–1948)**. Therefore a generic `J. Huizinga` bookplate is not a Johan-Huizinga provenance signal.

Source:
- Anton van der Lem, `Lees met beleid. Ex-librissen ontworpen door Johan Huizinga`, *Jaarboek van het Nederlands Genootschap van Bibliofielen* 23 (2015), p. 211 ff.: https://huizinga-online.nl/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Ex-librissen-Huizinga.pdf

### Consequence for the three Malinowski books

Image inspection should rank evidence as follows:

1. Malinowski presentation inscription naming Johan Huizinga;
2. `J. Huizinga` / `Johan Huizinga` in the upper-right title-page/cover position and hand compatible with recovered controls;
3. documented Huizinga-family or institutional chain of custody;
4. C.J.J.G. Vosmaer ex libris/number **only when paired with an independent Huizinga signal**;
5. a `J. Huizinga` ex libris alone is unsafe because Jakob used that name-form in bookplate provenance.

## 3. Public HUIZIN control remains negative for all three titles

The 2019 published list of recovered Huizinga books includes, among other controls:

- HUIZIN 2032 — Pirenne, gift F.M. Scholten-Huizinga;
- HUIZIN 2036 — Wensinck, carrying C.J.J.G. Vosmaer's ex libris;
- HUIZIN 2039 — Fruin, 11 vols., gift F.M. Scholten-Huizinga.

Exact searches of the published HUIZIN list and publicly indexed HUIZIN records did not produce `Malinowski`, `Sex and Repression`, `Crime and Custom`, or `Argonauts` as a recovered HUIZIN item in this pass.

This is only a **public-list/index negative**. It does not exclude later recoveries, uncatalogued material or a Huizinga copy sitting in a general institutional sequence.

Source:
- UBL / Huizinga Online, `Overzicht van de boeken uit het bezit van prof. dr. J. Huizinga, thans aanwezig in de UB Leiden`: https://huizinga-online.nl/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Lijst-van-teruggevonden-boeken.pdf

## 4. Family-retention branch: keep object-level batch test, not biographical speculation

F.M. Scholten-Huizinga/Fransje remains a real custody control because she demonstrably possessed Huizinga's Fruin set and donated identifiable Huizinga books to UBL. No public indexed hit in this pass connected any of the three Malinowski titles to `F.M. Scholten-Huizinga`, `Fransje Huizinga`, `Scholten-Huizinga`, Laura Conley-Huizinga, Jacoba de Jonge-de Meijere or Elisabeth Huizinga.

The useful next test remains the **donor/accession batch behind HUIZIN 2032 and 2039**: whether the associated donor documentation listed additional Huizinga books, transferred or not transferred to HUIZIN. Do not infer Malinowski custody from Leonhard's interests or the unverified p.146 recollection.

## 5. 1965 KB batch remains unresolved and should be tested only by exact title

Van der Lem states that Auguste Huizinga-Schölvinck donated about fifty Huizinga books to the Koninklijke Bibliotheek in 1965 and that the books were unfortunately not recorded by title in the account available to him. Public exact-title + Huizinga-Schölvinck/KB searches in this pass did not identify any of the three target copies.

Accordingly, this route is useful only if a 1965 accession/gift record can be queried directly for the three Malinowski titles or for a batch number that resolves to item records. Generic KB collection history adds nothing.

Source:
- Anton van der Lem, *Een bibliotheek als wederopstanding*: https://huizinga-online.nl/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Een-bibliotheek-als-wederopstanding.pdf
