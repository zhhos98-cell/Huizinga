# Malinowski KNAW 1933 membership-mechanics recheck — 2026-09-05

Purpose: publication-QA check of the May 1933 candidature sequence in Draft 04. Distinguish secure final membership status and Huizinga's own contemporaneous action from later claims about nomination/election agency.

## Secure contemporaneous pair evidence

Draft 04 already controls:

- Malinowski → Huizinga, 20 May 1933: thanks Huizinga for a letter and a reprint (public inventory incipit).
- Huizinga → Malinowski, 23 May 1933, correspondence item [1019]: `Being no anthropologist I refrained from taking a leading part in your candidature`, though Huizinga says he signed.

This supports exactly:

- a candidature was active;
- Huizinga signed in support;
- Huizinga explicitly says he did **not** take a leading part because he was not an anthropologist.

It does not by itself identify the formal proposer, seconder, nomination committee, vote, meeting date, or exact procedural route.

## Final membership status

The KNAW historical-member system identifies B.K. Malinowski under past-member ID `PE00001738`. Public sources citing that KNAW record consistently state that Malinowski became a **foreign member** of the Royal Netherlands Academy in 1933.

KNAW past-members entry point:
https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/pmknaw/

Historical-member ID:
`PE00001738`

The current KNAW detail URL is intermittently inaccessible through automated retrieval, so the member-type/year should be treated as secure database-level status while the procedural details remain open.

## Later historiographical claim

G.W. Locher later wrote that Malinowski `door de activiteit van de historicus Huizinga tot erelid van onze Kon. Akademie van Wetenschappen benoemd kon worden`.

Source:
G.W. Locher, `Nieuwe relaties tussen de culturele antropologie en de geschiedenis`, *Bijdragen en Mededelingen betreffende de Geschiedenis der Nederlanden* 83 (1968), DBNL.

This is valuable retrospective evidence about remembered Huizinga agency, but its wording (`erelid`) should not override the KNAW historical-member category (`foreign member`), and `door de activiteit ... Huizinga` should not be converted into exact nomination mechanics without the 1933 Academy file/minutes.
