# Leonhard Huizinga, *Herinneringen aan mijn vader*, p. 146: Malinowski locator — 2026-09-04

_Status: high-value source locator with unresolved context. Primary page not yet directly inspected._

## 1. Bžoch 2021 gives a precise claim and page locator

Adam Bžoch, `Johan Huizinga's Russia`, *Slovo a smysl / Word & Sense* 37 (2021), states that Leonhard Huizinga later recalled his father's discussions with Bronisław Malinowski **at the Huizinga house in Leiden**, during which conversation alternated between **Italian and Russian**. Bžoch cites Leonhard Huizinga, *Herinneringen aan mijn vader* (Den Haag: H.P. Leopold, 1963), **p. 146**.

Source:
- Adam Bžoch, `Johan Huizinga's Russia` (2021); searchable copy: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/357040871_Johan_Huizinga%27s_Russia

## 2. Serious contextual conflict: p. 146 lies inside the America-diary extract block

Michael Kammen, discussing Huizinga's 1926 American trip, explicitly directs readers to Leonhard Huizinga, *Herinneringen aan mijn vader*, **pp. 120–151** for extracts from the journal Huizinga kept during that journey.

The editors of Huizinga's *Briefwisseling* independently state that Huizinga kept a travel diary during the April–June 1926 American trip, and Anton van der Lem later published the complete surviving diary as *Amerika dagboek 14 april–19 juni 1926* (1993).

Therefore Bžoch's locator **p. 146 sits squarely inside the page range identified by Kammen as the 1926 America-diary extracts**.

Sources:
- Michael Kammen, `'This, Here, and Soon': Johan Huizinga's Esquisse of American Culture`, *BMGN* 97.3 (1982): https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_bij005198201_01/_bij005198201_01_0030.php
- *Briefwisseling*, vol. II: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b9/Briefwisseling%2C_Deel_II_1925-1933%2C_DigUBL.pdf
- Johan Huizinga, *Amerika dagboek 14 april–19 juni 1926*, ed. Anton van der Lem (Amsterdam/Antwerp: Contact, 1993).

## 3. Working interpretation

Bžoch's `Leiden house` setting must therefore remain **unverified and potentially displaced from an American-trip diary scene**. Three possibilities remain:

1. p. 146 is an America-diary passage about Malinowski in the United States, and Bžoch relocated it to Leiden;
2. Leonhard interrupts the diary extracts with a retrospective domestic recollection on or near p. 146;
3. Kammen's pp. 120–151 range is approximate and p. 146 belongs to surrounding commentary.

Only direct inspection of p. 146 can decide this.

## 4. Verification routes

1. Inspect *Herinneringen aan mijn vader* (1963), p. 146 directly. DBNL has a bibliographic record but currently provides no digitized text/scans for this title.
2. Search the complete 1993 *Amerika dagboek* for Malinowski and the Italian/Russian language detail. If the same passage appears there, the Leiden-house reading is disproved.
3. Check the 2011 German edition, Johan Huizinga, *Amerika: Mensch und Masse in Amerika | Amerika – Leben und Denken | Amerika-Tagebuch*, which contains the diary in translation.
4. Compare any primary 1926 diary manuscript/surrogate if accessible.
