# Ahlbrinck dispatch / receipt recheck — 15 September to 4 October 1931 — 2026-09-05

_Status: bounded publication-QA control. This note tests only whether the unidentified volume in Huizinga's 15 September 1931 Yale letter can be upgraded from HIGHLY PROBABLE to CERTAIN as W. Ahlbrinck's *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben*. It does not reopen generic correspondence or KNAW publication discovery._

## Controlled pair evidence

1. **15 September 1931 — Huizinga → Malinowski, Yale.** The public Huizinga correspondence inventory gives the incipit `I am sending you a volume just pubished`. The body/title remains uninspected in the repository. The folder locator is Bronislaw Malinowski Papers, MS 19, Series I: Correspondence, box 4, folder 280, `Huizinga, Johan, 1926–1933`, Yale University Library.
2. **4 October 1931 — Malinowski → Huizinga, item [910].** The next controlled pair letter thanks Huizinga for the *Encyclopaedia of the Karibs* and reports that a pupil staying with Malinowski and working on the couvade is already using it for additional information. The object is securely identifiable as W. Ahlbrinck, *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben: behelzend taal, zeden en gewoonten dezer Indianen* (KNAW, Afdeeling Letterkunde, Nieuwe Reeks XXVII.1, 1931).
3. Huizinga chaired the KNAW Afdeeling Letterkunde from 1929, giving the dispatch a plausible institutional channel. This establishes proximity, not personal editorial responsibility.

## What the September 2026 recheck changes

Earlier project notes counted the phrase `a volume just pubished` as an additional convergence with Ahlbrinck's 1931 book. That support is weaker than previously stated.

A bounded publication-history recheck found:

- the KNAW title/series record confirms **1931**, but not a September publication date;
- the KNAW digital volume identifies *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben* as Nieuwe Reeks XXVII.1 within a series volume spanning 1928–1931, which does not date issue-level release to September;
- Michiel van Kempen's documented Suriname publication history cites a *De Surinamer* review of the *Encyclopaedie* on **27 September 1931**, but also reports that L. Mannen had advertised the book for sale in Suriname on **1 January 1931**. Whatever the exact relation among printing, metropolitan issue, colonial sale and later review, this is enough to show that `just published` cannot independently date Ahlbrinck's first circulation to mid-September.

Therefore **publication timing must not be used as a positive identification criterion** for the 15 September dispatch.

## Revised evidentiary assessment

The linkage remains strong but inferential:

- **strong:** immediate pair chronology, 15 Sep dispatch → 4 Oct named receipt/use;
- **strong:** exact recipient and object class, a volume sent by Huizinga followed by Malinowski thanking him for a specific substantial volume;
- **supporting:** Huizinga's KNAW Letterkunde chairmanship supplies a plausible institutional route;
- **not supporting:** `just pubished` / publication timing, because the release history is not securely September and shows earlier circulation evidence.

Current status:

`15 SEP VOLUME = AHLBRINCK ENCYCLOPAEDIE DER KARAÏBEN` → **HIGHLY PROBABLE / NOTE-PARTIAL / NOT CERTAIN**

The secure circulation claim does **not** depend on this identification:

`Huizinga → Malinowski → pupil using Ahlbrinck for couvade` is directly controlled by the 4 October letter.

Only two source classes justify reopening the identification:

1. the full 15 September 1931 Yale letter body; or
2. an independent dispatch/presentation/accession record explicitly linking Huizinga, Malinowski and the Ahlbrinck volume.

Generic searches for Ahlbrinck's publication month, KNAW publication lists, or calendar adjacency should not be repeated.

## Article-facing decision

Keep the 15 September identification qualified as `highly probable` or equivalent. Remove any wording that treats the Ahlbrinck volume as demonstrably `newly published` in September or uses publication timing to strengthen the identification. The 4 October receipt/use scene remains BODY / SECURE.
