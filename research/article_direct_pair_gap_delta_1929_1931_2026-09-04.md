# Article-facing direct-pair gap calibration — 1929 / 1931 — 2026-09-04

_Status: bounded article-facing delta. This note recalibrates two unpublished Yale Huizinga→Malinowski letters already present in the correspondence inventory. It does not reopen generic correspondence discovery._

## 1. 23 April 1929: downgrade from presumed intellectual gap to continuity/personal-context control

The Yale correspondence inventory records an unpublished Johan Huizinga → Bronisław Malinowski letter dated **23 April 1929**, incipit:

> `You never let me hear anything`

The body remains uncontrolled in the current repository.

However, the editors of *Briefwisseling* independently cite this exact letter in a biographical note for the fact that Huizinga's son **Leonhard would perform his military service in Java in 1929**. This demonstrates that at least part of the letter contained family/personal news.

Primary control:
- *Briefwisseling*, vol. II, editorial note: `Leonhard Huizinga zou zijn dienstplicht in 1929 op Java vervullen, verg. Huizinga aan B. Malinowski, 23 april 1929.`
- Correspondence inventory: Huizinga → Malinowski, 23 Apr 1929, Yale, unpublished, incipit `You never let me hear anything`.

### Consequence for Draft 03

Do not treat the 23 April letter as a likely missing methodological/intellectual hinge merely because it sits between the 1928 and 13 May 1929 published exchanges.

Current evidential use:

- proves direct correspondence continued beyond the edited *Briefwisseling* selection;
- shows the relationship included personal/family news as well as scholarly exchange;
- remains potentially useful if the full Yale letter is easily obtained, but should not carry an active high-priority search burden.

## 2. 15 September 1931: strengthened dispatch→receipt inference

The Yale correspondence inventory records an unpublished Huizinga → Malinowski letter dated **15 September 1931**, incipit:

> `I am sending you a volume just pubished`

On **4 October 1931**, Malinowski opens his reply by thanking Huizinga for the **`Encyclopaedia of the Karibs`** and immediately reports that a pupil staying with him and working on the couvade is already using the book for additional information.

The book can be identified bibliographically as:

**W. Ahlbrinck, *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben: behelzend taal, zeden en gewoonten dezer Indianen*, illustrated by E. La Rose, Verhandelingen der Koninklijke Akademie van Wetenschappen te Amsterdam, Afdeeling Letterkunde, Nieuwe Reeks, deel XXVII, no. 1 (Amsterdam: Koninklijke Akademie van Wetenschappen, 1931).**

The KNAW title page confirms the 1931 publication and exact series position. Independent KNAW institutional history records **Johan Huizinga as chairman of the Afdeeling Letterkunde from 1929 to 1942** (more precisely from December 1929 in institutional-history literature).

Sources:
- Inventory of the Correspondence of Johan Huizinga: 15 Sep 1931 unpublished Yale letter, incipit `I am sending you a volume just pubished`.
- 4 Oct 1931 Malinowski → Huizinga letter [910], thanking him for the *Encyclopaedia of the Karibs* and describing its immediate use by a pupil studying couvade.
- KNAW digital publication: W. Ahlbrinck, *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben*, 1931: https://dwc.knaw.nl/DL/publications/PU00010187.pdf
- KNAW officials list: Afdeeling Letterkunde chairman, `1929–1942: J. Huizinga`: https://dwc.knaw.nl/en/biografie/knaw-voorzitters-sinds-1855/

### Evidential assessment

The identity of the 15 September dispatch is still **not textually proven**, because the surviving public inventory gives only the incipit and the letter body/title has not been inspected.

But the inference is materially stronger than simple calendar adjacency. Four controls converge:

1. **chronology:** 15 Sep dispatch → 4 Oct thanks, nineteen days later;
2. **object type:** `a volume just published` → a substantial new 1931 volume;
3. **exact recipient report:** Malinowski names the *Encyclopaedia of the Karibs* in his next controlled letter;
4. **institutional channel:** the volume was published by the KNAW Afdeeling Letterkunde while Huizinga chaired that department.

Current status:

`15 SEP VOLUME = AHLBRINCK ENCYCLOPAEDIE DER KARAÏBEN` → **HIGHLY LIKELY / NOT YET CERTAIN**

Do not upgrade to certainty without the 15 September letter body or an independent dispatch/presentation record.

## 3. Scene-IV consequence

This strengthens the first route in the 4 October circulation scene:

`Huizinga → Malinowski → Malinowski's pupil`

The route is already certain from the 4 October receipt/use letter. The new control makes the immediately preceding **dispatch event** highly likely to be identifiable as the 15 September unpublished Yale letter.

A compact article footnote can therefore say, in substance:

> The unpublished Yale inventory records Huizinga writing to Malinowski on 15 September 1931, `I am sending you a volume just pubished`. Malinowski's next controlled letter, 4 October, thanks him for W. Ahlbrinck's newly published *Encyclopaedie der Karaïben* (KNAW, Afdeeling Letterkunde, N.R. XXVII.1, 1931), already in use by a pupil working on couvade. Since Huizinga chaired the KNAW Afdeeling Letterkunde, the identification of the 15 September volume with Ahlbrinck is highly probable, although the unpublished letter body has not yet been inspected.

This is sufficient for a strong note. It does not justify claiming Huizinga personally edited, approved, selected, or otherwise managed Ahlbrinck's publication. Chairmanship establishes institutional proximity, not individual production responsibility.
