### Consequence for Draft 04

The present coda is chronologically wrong at this point: it narrates the 20/23 May 1933 KNAW candidature sequence and then begins the next paragraph `Later that year, in his rectoral address...`.

The rectoral address was **8 February 1933**, before the May candidature sequence.

Preferred repair: reorder the two substantive units chronologically rather than merely changing `Later` to `Earlier`:

1. 30 January Yale Dutch/*De Gids* letter;
2. 8 February rectoral address / Kula–potlatch–Burgundy;
3. 20/23 May reprint + KNAW candidature / non-leadership;
4. 27/30 October Hofstra exchange;
5. 6 November Amsterdam lecture / naming + return-to-the-past example.

The February address is already source-locked for the Kula/potlatch/Burgundy pages at *Verzamelde Werken* V, pp. 18–20.

## B. 6 November 1933 Amsterdam lecture: original title

The current Draft/References uses `Renaissance en nieuwe tijd` as though it were the publication title in *Neophilologus* 19 (1934): 81–95.

DBNL shows a bibliographic distinction that must be preserved.

The collected-works page is headed editorially:

`[Renaissance en Nieuwe Tijd]`

but the text immediately below is titled:

`Natuurbeeld en historiebeeld in de achttiende eeuw`.

The source note says:

- lecture delivered **6 November 1933** in the Aula of the University of Amsterdam for the Allard Pierson Stichting, Afdeeling voor Moderne Literatuurwetenschap;
- published in *Neophilologus*, vol. 19 (1934), pp. 81–95;
- also issued as no. 4 of the Allard Pierson Stichting series.

DBNL text:
https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huiz003verz05_01/huiz003verz05_01_0048.php

DBNL bibliography, *Verzamelde Werken* IX, item 226, confirms the publication title and event:

`NATUURBEELD EN HISTORIEBEELD IN DE ACHTTIENDE EEUW` — Groningen/Den Haag/Batavia, Wolters, 1933; lecture delivered 6 Nov. 1933 in Amsterdam; separate issue is an offprint from *Neophilologus*.

Bibliography URL:
https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/huiz003verz09_01/huiz003verz09_01_0005.php

### Consequence for Draft 04

The current body sentence `On 6 November, in an Amsterdam lecture on Renaissance and 'new time'...` and the References entry `Huizinga, J. (1934) 'Renaissance en nieuwe tijd', Neophilologus 19: 81–95` conflate the collected-works editorial heading with the original published title.

Repair:

- body: call it the 6 November Amsterdam lecture `Natuurbeeld en historiebeeld in de achttiende eeuw` (optionally note that the collected works places it under `[Renaissance en Nieuwe Tijd]` only if needed; probably unnecessary in body);
- References: `Huizinga, J. (1934) 'Natuurbeeld en historiebeeld in de achttiende eeuw', Neophilologus 19: 81–95.`
- keep the naming passage at *Verzamelde Werken* IV, p. 341;
- keep the Hofstra/Willoughby footnote relation from the same text.
