## p0131-p0132 — `Vorstelijk Kaartspel`

This is the strongest play/game hit in chunk 026. `p0131` is headed approximately `Vorstelijk Kaartspel`; `p0132` gives `Vorstelyk Kaartspel tusschen een Spanjaard, Hollander, Fransman, Engelsman en Hoogduitser`. ## p0240-p0254 — learned letters and the Grotius editorial project

`p0240` is a 1908 Maatschappij der Nederlandsche Letterkunde circular about P. C. Molhuysen's catalogue of printed letters to and from Dutch scholars, described as containing more than 40,000 slips. `p0241-p0251` supplies the associated bibliography.

`p0253-p0254` prints the statutes of the `Vereeniging voor de uitgave van Grotius`. Its purpose is to gather the complete material of Grotius's letters and works and publish it under the direction of the board. The statute is dated 2 January 1917; `J. Huizinga` appears among the signatories with C. van Vollenhoven, P. C. Molhuysen, A. Eekhof, G. Vissering, D. F. Scheurleer and G. J. Fabius. This is direct institutional evidence, not merely a bibliographical association.
