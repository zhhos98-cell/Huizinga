## Tier 1 — visual/material production and circulation

### `chunk_013:p0141–p0148` — Reformation/Luther working sequence

This is a high-value documentary cluster because handwritten working notes are immediately interleaved with externally produced visual and bibliographical objects.

- `p0141–p0142`: mounted handwritten slips; `p0142` visibly includes **“Ridderspelen als beleving der Feodaltijd.”**
- `p0143`: publisher advertisement for **Museum der Weltgeschichte**, with **Textabbildungen** and black/colour plates.
- `p0144`: printed **Martin Luther** portrait after a **1521 Lucas Cranach the Elder engraving**, paired with a handwritten slip.
- `p0145`: advertisement for Hartmann Grisar, *Martin Luthers Leben und sein Werk*, advertised with **13 Tafeln**.
- `p0146`: *Luthers Kampfbilder*, explicitly organized around **Abbildungen**, **Bilder im Text**, **Tafeln**, and image conflict in pamphlets.
- `p0147–p0148`: Karl Schottenloher bibliographical prospectus, including a projected historical map and fascicle-based publication plan.

Research value: the archive preserves not just Huizinga-authored prose but a production chain of publishers, engravers/reproduction traditions, advertisements, bibliographical projects, maps/plates, and mounted slips. That materially complicates any attempt to treat “Huizinga's images” as a single authorial visual corpus.

### `chunk_013:p0167–p0211` — XVIIIe-eeuw lecture sequence

`p0211` labels the run **“XVIII e eeuw (ter uitwerken)”**. Several pages are first-order evidence for institutions, media, reproduction and diffusion:

- `p0167`: **bibliotheken, kabinetten, galerij, magazijn, museum, kunsthandel**.
- `p0169`: representation/style vocabulary; **Swift–Hogarth** and encyclopedism.
- `p0174`: engraving/illustration in the English visual-material cluster.
- `p0200`: **panorama** of nature and society; historical **wereldbeeld**; images interweaving.
- `p0207`: **Prentkunst** as illustration/decoration/reproduction; galleries/magazines; art academies; theatre; opera/concert; **publiciteit**; instruments/material means.
- `p0209`: explicit distinction between **product** and **diffusie**; visual art and the **République des lettres**.

Research value: these pages supply Huizinga's own working vocabulary for how cultural forms are institutionalized, reproduced, made public, and diffused. `p0207` and `p0209` should be close-transcribed later.
