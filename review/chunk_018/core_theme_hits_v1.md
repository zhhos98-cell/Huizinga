## Tier 1 — analogy, visual practice, and documentary organization

### `chunk_018:p0147–p0150` — William James clipping, analogy, and handwritten processing

`p0147` is a clean printed clipping headed **`UIT DE PHILOSOPHIE VAN WILLIAM JAMES`**. In the quoted discussion of automatisms and subliminal consciousness it contains the explicit methodological phrase **`Het onbekende verklarende naar analogie van het bekende`** (explaining the unknown by analogy with the known). `p0148` continues the printed James discussion and is physically paired with a separate handwritten note; `p0149–p0150` continue with handwritten notes on pragmatism, truth, and related thinkers.

Research value: this is a strong **analogy-family** witness inside Huizinga's working papers. The safe claim is archival: a Dutch printed exposition/quotation of James that explicitly formulates inference by analogy was retained and placed alongside handwritten processing. The phrase itself belongs to the printed James discussion, not automatically to Huizinga.
